# 01. Partner-type landscape: channels into rural and community hospitals

Research date: 2026-09-24. Eight research threads ran in parallel. Their full notes, with every source URL, are in `output/work/phase1/` (files A to H). This file is the synthesis. Every factual line carries a source. "(judgment)" marks our own reasoning. "Per <org>" marks an organization's claim about itself. Phase 5 re-checked the load-bearing claims, and its corrections are applied here and noted inline.

## How to read this

- **Motions:** R = refer, E = endorse, RS = resell/bundle, I = integrate/marketplace, C = contract vehicle, F = fund/subsidize.
- **Reach** is the realistic number of rural, critical access (CAH) or community hospitals a single partner of this type touches.
- **Speed** is time to a first signed hospital, not to a signed partnership (judgment).
- **Competitor risk** follows the brief's test. It is high if the partner sells its own denials or appeals product, or fully outsourced revenue cycle that includes denials.

## Five findings that change the plan

1. **Both of Aegis's EHRs now draft appeals themselves.**
   - MEDITECH launched "Claim Denial Agents" at HIMSS26 (2026-03-10). Per MEDITECH, the agent "drafts an evidence-based appeal with clinical data extracted from the EHR" ([BusinessWire](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20260310271908/en/MEDITECHs-Latest-AI-Innovations-Support-Smarter-Care-and-Stronger-Performance)).
   - Oracle Health announced an "Appeal management" tool on 2026-09-23 that "automates appeal packet creation", with general availability "in the coming months" ([Becker's](https://beckershospitalreview.com/finance/revenue-cycle-management/oracle-health-targets-denials-with-5-new-revenue-cycle-ai-tools)).
   - Consequence: the EHR marketplace motion is now partner-and-competitor at once. Every channel partner will ask "why not the EHR's agent?" (judgment).
2. **The CMS Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) is real and funds denials tools in a few states.**
   - Size: $50B over FY2026-2030 under P.L. 119-21 Sec. 71401. CMS announced FY2026 awards for all 50 states on 2025-12-29, from $147.3M (NJ) to $281.3M (TX) ([CMS](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-50-billion-awards-strengthen-rural-health-all-50-states)).
   - Minnesota's hospital grants explicitly allow revenue cycle tools that reduce "claim denials", and hospitals pick their own vendors ([MDH NOGO](https://www.web.health.state.mn.us/facilities/ruralhealth/ruraltrans/docs/hospitalnogo.pdf)).
   - Kansas funds a state revenue-cycle support organization, run through the hospital association's Healthworks, that will "pursue payment recovery on denied and unpaid claims" ([KDHE budget narrative](https://www.kdhe.ks.gov/DocumentCenter/View/56710/RHT-Plan-Updated-Budget-Narrative-2026-PDF)).
   - Montana plans state-facilitated shared back-office vendors, including revenue cycle, where "Vendors offering favorable rates to leverage the latest AI enabled technology, with a clearly demonstrated history of success with similar-sized providers, will be prioritized" ([MT RHTP plan](https://dphhs.mt.gov/assets/RuralHealthTransformation/RHTP-Plan.pdf)).
   - Scope: this is a lever in roughly 10 states, not 50. The window is now through Q1 2027 (details in section F1).
3. **One firm quietly runs the association endorsement machinery.**
   - Affiliated Enterprise Solutions (AES) says it builds and runs endorsed-partner programs for "over 45 Healthcare Associations" ([AES](https://www.aesllc.org/solutions/association-solutions/)).
   - Its President and CEO, Brian Stevens, is the listed endorsed-business-partner contact for Michigan's program ([MHA](https://mha.org/business-services/endorsed-business-partner-program)) and New Hampshire's ([NHHA](https://nhha.org/membership/endorsed-partners)).
   - Consequence: one conversation could open a vetting path into many states (judgment).
4. **A live group contract for denials appeals exists in Washington.**
   - Rural Health Enterprise, the business arm of The Rural Collaborative (31 hospitals, 28 of them CAHs), issued a March 2026 RFP whose required scope includes "Appeal letter preparation, Submission of appeals". It asks for group pricing and an "RHE master services agreement, inclusive of program support administration fee" ([RFP PDF](https://ruralcollaborative.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RHE-RFP-contract-compliance-denial-mgmt-credit-bal.pdf)).
   - The award status was not found.
5. **Aegis's own customer sits inside several Montana channels.**
   - Glendive Medical Center is listed as a shareholder of Montana Health Network, a hospital-owned insurance and shared-services company ([MHN](https://montanahealthnetwork.com/about-us)).
   - It is also a member of the Yellowstone High Value Network, 24 Montana CAHs managed by Cibolo Health ([YHVN](https://yellowstonenetwork.org/networks)).
   - Aegis is itself already a Wisconsin Hospital Association "Corporate - Associate" member, listed under Revenue Cycle Management ([WHA directory](https://wha.org/about-wha/corporate-membership/corporate-member-directory)).

## A. Hospital associations and their endorsement machinery

### A1. State hospital association service arms

- **How hospitals buy through it.**
  - Most associations own a for-profit subsidiary that vets vendors, signs a contract with member pricing, and markets the vendor with endorsement letters, events and intros. Hospitals still buy directly from the vendor.
  - Examples: THA Innovative Solutions (TN) ([process](https://thainnovativesolutions.com/process/)). Washington Hospital Services: "When your hospital uses one of our services, the revenue supports the Washington State Hospital Association" ([WHS PDF](https://wahospitalservices.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/WHS-Preferred-Partners.pdf)).
- **Economics (public figures only).**
  - Tennessee: $25,000/yr plus revenue share, with category exclusivity ([THA IS](https://thainnovativesolutions.com/become-a-partner/)).
  - New Mexico HSC: "the greater of (i) a monthly marketing fee ... or (ii) a negotiated percentage of the fees", with a 12-month tail after termination ([HSC terms](https://docest.com/doc/595811/hospital-services-corporation)).
  - Maryland: "Revenue Share with a commitment to sponsor MHA Annual Meeting" ([MHA Prime](https://mhaonline.org/about-maryland-hospital-association/partnerships)).
  - Oklahoma: 16 partners produced about $180k/yr of non-dues revenue in 2023 ([OHA 2023 report](https://www.okoha.com/Common/Uploaded%20files/ohadocs/publications%20and%20resources/annual%20reports/2023%20ANNUAL%20REPORT.pdf)).
  - Tax case (2017): a collection agency paid the NJ Council of Teaching Hospitals 3% plus 3% of collections ([Tax Notes](https://www.taxnotes.com/research/federal/court-documents/court-opinions-and-orders/fees-from-third-party-vendors-are-ubti-to-tax-exempt/1xfvf)).
  - No association publishes an exact royalty percentage.
- **Reach.** CAH counts per state (Flex Monitoring Team, 2024 cost reports): MT 49, NE 61, SD 39, MN 75, IA 81, KS 82, ND 37, WA 39, WI 53, IL 53 ([FMT](https://flexmonitoring.org/sites/flexmonitoring.umn.edu/files/media/state-medians-2024data_report-final_2026.pdf)). The Texas rural association TORCH says it has 157 rural members ([TORCH speaker page](https://texlatrc.org/2026-digital-health-summit/meet-the-speakers/)).
- **Speed.** Three to nine months to endorsement. Tennessee reviews applicants at a quarterly board meeting and accepts about 2% ([process](https://thainnovativesolutions.com/process/)). The first hospital deal likely follows within 1-2 quarters (judgment).
- **Competitor risk: the denials slot is taken in some states.**

  | Status | States (sourced in work files C and D) |
  |---|---|
  | **Taken by a denials or appeals vendor** | KY (CorroHealth; FairCode appeal letters), MS (Trilogy RCM exclusive; CorroHealth/PARA), TX-TORCH (TruBridge, Meduit, SlicedHealth), OK (Coronis), MI (CorroHealth; SmarterDx appeal letters, Nov 2025), CO (LateralCare AI denials, Apr 2026; Ovation), NJ (Jzanus), HASC-CA (CorroHealth), MN (CorroHealth, ModusOne, CommerceHealthcare), ND (CorroHealth), MT (CorroHealth listed for "Revenue cycle management") |
  | **CorroHealth endorsed but scoped to price transparency** (denials arguably open) | IA ([ServiShare](https://servishareonline.org/service/price-transparency)), KS ([KHSC](https://kha-net.org/AboutKHA/KHSC/KHSCEndorsedServices/CorroHealth)), IN ([IHA](https://partners.ihaconnect.org/_corrohealth)) |
  | **Listed as endorsed in 2023 but not on the current list** | SD ([SDAHO endorsed list](https://enterprises.sdaho.org/endorsed-partners)) |
  | **No denials vendor named** (open slot, judgment) | WA, WY, WI, PA, NY (HANYS, Iroquois), ME, NH, IL, MA, MD, TN, AR, NC, SC, WV, AL, GA, TX-THA, NM, VA |
  | **No vendor program** | OR, UT, NV (association), AK; AZ program suspended |

- **Motions.** Endorse (primary), refer, contract (Mississippi's MHA Solutions is treated as a GPO for public hospitals), fund (association foundations).
- **Vetting criteria found.**
  - WHS (WA): SME review, background and viability review, calls to other state associations, hospital reviews, references, board ([WHS PDF](https://wahospitalservices.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/WHS-Preferred-Partners.pdf)).
  - MHA Ventures (MT): RFI, demo, financial health check, customer references ([MHA Ventures](https://mtha.org/business-solutions/mha-ventures-inc/endorsed-services)).
  - NM HSC: other association endorsements, and client lists in NM/TX/CO/AZ ([HSC](https://docest.com/doc/595811/hospital-services-corporation)).
  - Indiana: discounts to members as a condition of endorsement ([IHA](https://partners.ihaconnect.org/_prospectivepartners)).
  - Many programs are exclusive per category: "We only choose one partner per service" ([WHS](https://wahospitalservices.com/preferred-partners)).
- **Example orgs.** Washington Hospital Services (39 WA CAHs; no denials vendor named). THA Innovative Solutions (TN; transparent terms). HAPevolve (PA). Iroquois Healthcare Association (NY rural). Maine Hospital Solutions.
- **Surprise.** Virginia's VHHA Solutions endorsed an agentic-AI platform (Andor Health) in Feb 2026, a sign these channels will endorse AI ([C file](work/phase1/C_assoc_set1.md)). South Carolina's association created Qualivis, which is now endorsed by 27 associations, showing a vendor can scale state by state ([GHA](https://www.gha.org/GHHSEndorsedServices)).

### A2. Association program operators: Affiliated Enterprise Solutions (AES)

- **What it does.**
  - Per AES, it builds and runs endorsement programs for "over 45 Healthcare Associations". Named clients include the ND, SD, KS, IA, WI and NM hospital associations, the National Rural Health Resource Center and the NC State Office of Rural Health ([AES](https://www.aesllc.org/solutions/association-solutions/)).
  - AES labels HASC, WSHA, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Maine, ND, SD, Vermont and Wisconsin as past clients ([AES](https://aesllc.org/solutions/association-solutions)). Whether each program is still AES-run needs checking per state (judgment).
- **Motions.** Endorse (meta: one vetting, many lists). AES also sells "channel partner development" and "introductions to decision makers" to vendors ([AES](https://aesllc.org/solutions/aes-healthcare-business-solutions)), so it may also be a paid door.
- **Competitor risk.** Medium. Its portfolio lists "Rev Cycle / Managed Zero Balance & Underpayment Recovery", which is recovery, not appeals ([AES](https://www.aesllc.org/)).
- **People.** Brian Stevens and Matthew Bobo are already in the v1 seed.

### A3. National Rural Health Association (NRHA) Services Corp

- **Terms.** The "Pipeline Partnership" costs $20,000/yr. An "Emerging Partner" must have at least 3 rural implementations, and a rural hospital committee vets partners ([NRHA info sheet](https://nrhapartners.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/NRHA-Rural-Hospital-Partnership-Info-Sheet-7.pdf)).
- **Reach.** Per NRHA, "1,900+" hospitals are reached (same source).
- **Taken categories.** Current partners already include RCM firms: InlandRCM, Net Revenue, Azalea, Demi, HELP Financial and Eide Bailly ([NRHA SC](https://nrhasc.com/meet-your-partners/)).
- **Person.** Larry Bedell is in the seed.

## B. Networks

### B1. Rural health networks, CAH networks and co-ops

- **How hospitals buy through it.** These are hospital-owned or hospital-governed collaboratives. They run CFO and revenue cycle peer groups, negotiate vendor contracts, and sometimes run shared services. Members opt in per contract; Medi-Sota says "no contracts or services are mandatory" ([Medi-Sota](https://medi-sota.org/main/index.php/en/membership-benefits)). NCHN, their national association, has about 40 network members and a paid Business Partner program ([NCHN prospectus](https://nchn.org/site/business-partner-prospectus)).
- **Economics.**
  - Admin fees and revenue shares are normal. The RHE RFP requires an "administration fee" ([RFP](https://ruralcollaborative.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Request-For-Proposal-Hospital-and-Clinic-Billing-Services-Final-031926.pdf)).
  - ICAHN "may receive important financial support as services are utilized" (2021, stale) ([ICAHN report](https://icahn.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/2021-ICAHN-Annual-Report.pdf)).
  - Grants flow through networks too. Washington's RHTP application proposes $5-6M/yr to The Rural Collaborative for revenue cycle capacity and AI tools ([WA narrative](https://hca.wa.gov/assets/program/rhtp-project-narrative.pdf)).
- **Reach.**
  - Heartland Health Alliance (Bryan) 59.
  - ICAHN 60 organizations.
  - Prairie Health Ventures 50+.
  - Medi-Sota 37.
  - The Rural Collaborative 31 (28 CAHs).
  - Western Healthcare Alliance 31.
  - Sources in [E file](work/phase1/E_networks_systems.md).
- **Speed.** 1-3 months for education slots at revenue cycle peer groups. 3-6 months for a preferred-vendor listing. 6-12 months for network master agreements (judgment).
- **Competitor risk.**
  - Low for peer-group and insurance networks.
  - High where the network sells RCM:
    - ICAHN lists OS inc. for "denial management" ([ICAHN](https://icahn.org/business-partners/our-sponsors-and-partners/preferred-partners)).
    - The New England Collaborative Health Network is run by Ovation ([NECHN](https://nechnetwork.com/who-we-are)).
    - Great Plains Health Alliance runs a central business office with Oracle Health, paid as a % of collections ([GPHA](https://gpha.com/services)).
- **Example orgs.** The Rural Collaborative / RHE (WA), Western Healthcare Alliance (CO/UT), Medi-Sota (MN), ICAHN (IL), Nevada Rural Hospital Partners (13 CAHs; runs a revenue-cycle improvement program that includes denials management) ([NRHP](https://nrhp.org/what-we-do/revenue-cycle-improvement-program/)).

### B2. Rural clinically integrated networks (Cibolo Health "high value networks")

- **Reach.** Seven networks and about 124 hospitals, summing the counts on Cibolo's site: ND 23, MN 17, MT 24, OH 24, WI 10, NE 19, KS 7 ([Cibolo](https://cibolohealth.com/networks)). A separate 23-hospital Missouri network launched in Sept 2026; its manager was not found ([MHA](https://mohospitals.org/newsroom/independent-missouri-hospitals-unite-to-launch-show-me-high-value-network)).
- **Competitor risk: high.** Cibolo's services "include ... denial management", and it chose TruBridge as preferred RCM partner ([BusinessWire](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20250212546655/en/TruBridge-Selected-by-Cibolo-Health-as-a-Preferred-Partner-for-RCM-Technology-and-Services)).
- **Why it still matters (judgment).** Glendive is a Yellowstone HVN member. The networks are hospital-governed, so a board or CFO committee can still pick a denials-only tool that sits next to TruBridge.

### B3. Hospital-owned shared-service and insurance networks

- **Montana Health Network (Miles City).**
  - Sells liability, malpractice and workers' comp insurance, staffing, education and credentialing ([MHN business services](https://montanahealthnetwork.com/business-services)).
  - Its listed shareholders include Glendive Medical Center, Billings Clinic, Central Montana Medical Center, Daniels Memorial, Sidney Health Center and about 12 other Montana hospitals ([MHN](https://montanahealthnetwork.com/about-us)). It says it serves "approximately 40 other healthcare entities".
  - No revenue cycle product was found, so competitor risk is low.
- **Peers in other states:** Kentucky Hospital Insurance Company RRG ([KHA](https://www.kyha.com/kha-solutions-group/kentucky-hospital-insurance-company/)) and New Mexico's Yellowstone Insurance Exchange ([HSC](https://www.nmhospitalservices.com/yellowstone)).

## C. Funders

### C1. CMS Rural Health Transformation Program (state agencies and intermediaries)

- **What it is.** $10B per year for FY2026-2030. Half is split equally and half by CMS scoring. Only states receive money; "Local governments, hospitals ... may still be able to participate through subawards" ([CMS FAQ](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/rural-health-transformation-frequently-asked-questions.pdf)).
- **Caps.** Provider payments ≤15%, EMR replacement ≤5%, capital ≤20% ([NOFO](https://apply07.grants.gov/apply/opportunities/instructions/PKG00291485-instructions.pdf)). No supplanting, meaning existing software licenses cannot be paid ([MN NOGO](https://www.web.health.state.mn.us/facilities/ruralhealth/ruraltrans/docs/hospitalnogo.pdf)).
- **Deadlines.** Budget-period-1 money must be obligated by 2026-10-30 and spent by 2027-09-30. Unspent money is redistributed ([NRHA NOFO summary](https://nrha-prod-eastus-be.azure.silvertech.net/NationalRuralHealth/media/Documents/Advocacy/2025/RHTP-NOFO-summary-9-16-25.pdf)). FY2027 amounts are set by 2026-10-31.
- **How hospitals buy through it.** Three patterns:
  1. **The hospital gets a subgrant and picks its vendor.** MN formula grants (about 94 hospitals, up to $1.4M each in budget period 1). WA Provider Technology Fund ($200-500k awards) ([HCA RFA](https://www.hca.wa.gov/assets/program/2026hca12-rfa-notice-of-request-for-applications.pdf)). MS Rural Provider Technology Grants: objective 5 names "optimizing revenue cycle"; $200-400k awards ([MS NOFO](https://mississippirhtp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ada_2026-MS-RTG-Program-NOFO-Version-2-061726.pdf)). Also ID, ND, SD and WY.
  2. **The state or an intermediary runs a shared service.**
     - Kansas's revenue-cycle support organization, run via Healthworks. Its RFPs closed in July 2026, and Healthworks' objectives include "Assisting hospitals with appeals" ([Healthworks](https://www.kha-net.org/aboutkha/healthworks/projects/rhtp/)).
     - Montana's Center of Excellence implementation contractor, which will "Facilitate vendor access at preferred rates for back-office ... tools via existing or new GPO contract" (~$1.23M) ([MT budget narrative](https://dphhs.mt.gov/assets/RuralHealthTransformation/RHTPBudgetNarrative.pdf)).
     - Oklahoma's provider collaborative ($40M; shared admin services) ([OSDH](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/health/health2/aem-documents/health-promotion/rhtp/webinar/20260421%20RHTP%20Virtual%20Touchpoint%20vFinal.pdf)).
  3. **Startup catalyst funds** (NE NETECH, GA ARCHER). These are weak fits because they favor clinical or consumer tech.
- **Evidence money has reached denials work.** Arkansas funded Delta Memorial Hospital's "denial management" revenue cycle project for $24,720 ([AR Governor](https://governor.arkansas.gov/news_post/sanders-announces-nearly-150-million-awarded-in-rural-health-transformation-funds/)).
- **Competitor risk: medium-high.**
  - Waystar ([blog](https://www.waystar.com/blog-funding-for-rural-healthcare-how-to-use-rhtp-funds/)) and FinThrive ([blog](https://finthrive.com/blog/protecting-rural-care-navigating-the-50-billion-dollar-rhtp)) market RHTP-funded denials tools. Quadax and SlicedHealth do the same.
  - ND picked Eide Bailly as preferred vendor for rightsizing assessments, which include revenue cycle ([ND HHS](https://www.hhs.nd.gov/sites/default/files/documents/rhtp/rightsizing-ta-call-handout-certified.pdf)).
  - Kansas is building a subsidized appeals team, which could substitute for Aegis.
- **Vetting.** No federal approved-vendor list exists. Colorado: "Vendors may only participate as subcontractors or partners under provider-led applications" ([HCPF FAQ](https://hcpf.colorado.gov/sites/hcpf/files/RHTP%20Applicant%20FAQs%20%28for%20publication%29.pdf)). South Dakota: "No vendor applications are being accepted" ([SD DOH](https://doh.sd.gov/healthcare-professionals/rural-health/rural-health-transformation-project/rht-resources-faqs/)).
- **Speed (judgment).** 3-9 months, gated by budget-period-2 work plans (from 2026-10-31) and second-round solicitations (e.g., WI hospitals in 2027).
- The full 50-state table is in [A_rhtp.md](work/phase1/A_rhtp.md).

### C2. State Offices of Rural Health, Flex and SHIP

- **How hospitals buy through it.** Every state Flex program must run "CAH Financial & Operational Improvement" work each year. HRSA's example revenue cycle outcomes include "Reduced percentage of claims denied" and an increased percentage of denied claims re-billed ([Flex program structure FY24-28](https://www.ruralcenter.org/sites/default/files/FY24-FY28%20Flex%20Program%20Structure.pdf)). This is close to Aegis's own metric. Money reaches vendors in three ways:
  1. **A state RFP or RFQ.** Hawaii's Flex office issued an RFQ in May 2026 for a "Critical Access Hospital Claims Denial Dashboard", not to exceed $18,000 ([GovCB](https://www.govcb.com/government-bids/CRITICAL-ACCESS-HOSPITAL-CLAIMS-AND-23616862.htm)).
  2. **Per-CAH subgrants the hospital uses to hire help.** North Dakota subcontracts include an $11,550 revenue cycle review ([UND CRH](https://ruralhealth.und.edu/projects/flex/funding-programs/awards)). Wisconsin gives up to $15,000 per hospital for revenue cycle consultants ([WORH](https://worh.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/D2H-Finance-Analysis-Grant-2025-26.pdf)).
  3. **A standing contractor that runs the financial improvement work.**
     - Iowa uses HomeTown Health, to "reduce risk and costs related to managing denials" ([Iowa HHS](https://hhs.iowa.gov/health-prevention/providers-professionals/rural-health/rural-hospital-programs)).
     - Kansas runs a KRHOP cohort whose goals include "Reduce the rate of claims denials" ([KRHOP](https://krhop.net/projects/efficient-revenue-cycle-processes)).
     - Montana uses MHREF/MHA, which ran a Revenue Cycle Intensive for 32 CAH staff from 17 facilities ([MT PIN](https://mtpin.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-Quality-Update.pdf)).
     - Virginia builds a vetted consultant portfolio in which "technology-enabled operational improvement projects" qualify ([VRHA](https://vrha.org/flex/)).
- **SHIP.** About $13,528 per hospital in FY2026 ([MN SHIP NOGO](https://www.health.mn.gov/facilities/ruralhealth/funding/grants/docs/shipnogo.pdf)).
  - Software subscriptions and revenue cycle training are allowable. "Contractors, Consulting" including "Vendor Consultation" are not (per the SHIP allowability search tool, [B file](work/phase1/B_sorh_flex_funders.md)).
  - Oregon's SHIP pooled menu already offers Wipfli "Denials Analysis and Education" ([OHSU](https://www.ohsu.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/FY25%20Pooled%20Options%20SHIP%20-%20Final.pdf)).
  - Judgment: a per-claim appeals service probably does not qualify. A software-plus-training package might, with HRSA project officer sign-off.
- **Economics.** FY2026 Flex appropriation is $70.3M including $22.9M for SHIP ([NRHA CAA 2026 summary](https://nrha-prod-eastus-be.azure.silvertech.net/NationalRuralHealth/media/Documents/Advocacy/2026/CAA-2026-rural-health-summary.pdf)). Ticket sizes are small ($10-20k). This is a pilot and credibility channel, not a revenue channel (judgment).
- **Reach.** All CAHs in a state (about 1,350 nationally). SORHs are also RHTP intermediaries in some states: Colorado's Rural Health Center gives RHTP application TA ([HCPF](https://hcpf.colorado.gov/rural-health-transformation-program)).
- **Competitor risk.** Low. SORHs sell nothing. The financial improvement slots are held by consultants (HomeTown Health, Wipfli, Eide Bailly, Stroudwater, Wintergreen, ArchPro), not AI appeals vendors.
- **Speed (judgment).** 6-12 months. Flex budgets follow HRSA's Sept-to-Aug year.
- **NOSORH.** Investing Partner tiers run from $4,000 to $15,000+ ([NOSORH](https://nosorh.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/2026-NOSORH-Investing-Partner-Opportunity-Chart.pdf)).
- **Best targets (judgment).** MT, ND, HI, VA, OR, KS.
- A 50-state list of SORHs and Flex leads is in [B_sorh_flex_funders.md](work/phase1/B_sorh_flex_funders.md). Most coordinator names come from undated TASC profiles, so check them before use.

### C3. Other funders

- **Programs that can pay for denials work.**
  - HRSA's Delta Health Systems Implementation Program gives grants of up to $400,000 to implement technical-assistance recommendations, including "revenue cycle best practices" ([HRSA-25-032](https://www.hrsa.gov/grants/find-funding/HRSA-25-032)).

- **Programs that are a poor fit.** USDA Community Facilities loans and grants cover rural health facilities, equipment and professional fees ([USDA](https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/community-facilities)). CoBank and Helmsley fund capital and equipment. Lenders finance capital, not software choices, so fit is low (judgment).

## H. Contract vehicles

### H1. National GPOs

- **Fees.** The anti-kickback safe harbor allows vendor fees of "3 percent or less" under a written agreement ([42 CFR 1001.952(j)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-V/subchapter-B/part-1001/subpart-C/section-1001.952)). GAO found actual weighted averages of 1.22-2.25% ([GAO](https://gao.gov/assets/gao-10-738.pdf)).
- **HealthTrust barrier.** Its supplier criteria include "national in scope" distribution and sales force ([HealthTrust](https://healthtrustpg.com/dl/EC.HT.010.pdf)).
- **Intalere is gone.** Vizient acquired it on 2021-04-01 ([Vizient](https://vizient.com/newsroom/news-releases/vizient-completes-acquisition-of-intalere-expands-its-supply-chain-capabilities)).
- **Premier-aligned.** AllSpire, Acurity, Yankee, CHAMPS, Prairie Health Ventures, IPC (Illinois) and MHA Ventures (MT) all buy through Premier ([G file](work/phase1/G_tech_ehr_gpo.md)).
- **Taken.** No national GPO denials-appeals award was found. Premier holds a "revenue cycle solutions" category, and R1 is resold via Premier (per brief).

### H2. Regional purchasing co-ops and network RFPs

- **Example.** RHE (WA) is the live example (see finding 4). Its RFP requires CAH references (3), Washington payer experience, a BAA, insurance, and a statement of whether denial work happens "directly within the host EHR" ([RFP](https://ruralcollaborative.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Request-For-Proposal-Hospital-and-Clinic-Billing-Services-Final-031926.pdf)).
- **Speed (judgment).** 2-6 months. This is the realistic contract vehicle for a small vendor.

## What surprised us

1. The EHR vendors moved first on AI appeal drafting (finding 1).
2. Aegis is already a Wisconsin Hospital Association corporate member.
3. AES is a single door into the endorsement programs of many rural-state associations.
4. Kansas is using federal money to build a state-subsidized appeals team, making it a channel and a competitor at once.
5. Glendive Medical Center co-owns a Montana hospital network.
6. Two denials incumbents moved in 2025-26:
   - Curi, a malpractice insurer, now sells RCM.
   - Colorado's association endorsed LateralCare's AI denials tool in Apr 2026.
7. AI appeals competitors are already recruiting consultants. Aerolib runs a three-tier channel program ([Aerolib](https://aerolib.ai/channel-partner)).
8. Anti-kickback question (inference, needs counsel):
   - The issue: CAHs are paid on reported cost, so percentage referral fees tied to hospital purchases could draw scrutiny. That is why the GPO safe harbor exists.
   - The safer structures: flat fair-market-value association fees, or GPO-style written agreements ([H file](work/phase1/H_finance_insurers_events_econ.md)).
