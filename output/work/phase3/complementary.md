## Dropped and why
- MedCycle Solutions: sells "Rejection & Denial Management" and "Appeals" (https://medcyclesolutions.com/certified-medical-coder-biller-2/). This makes it a competitor, not a complement.
- HealthDox: its KHSC endorsement is for policy, risk and compliance (GRC) software, not price transparency, and the buyer is compliance/quality rather than the CFO (https://www.kha-net.org/AboutKHA/KHSC/KHSCEndorsedServices/HealthDox/).
- Strata Decision: skews to large systems per the task brief. No rural-specific search was run, so it is NOT_FOUND for rural reach.
- AccessOne, iVitaFi, RevSpring, TailorMed, Corpay, Paymerang: no rural association endorsement or rural reach was verified in this pass. Candidates for a later pass. (RevSpring and PatientPay are TORCH corporate members per the phase 1 C file.)

## Key findings / surprises
- Multiview ERP is the strongest structural fit. Cibolo Health named it exclusive preferred ERP across six rural HVNs, including Montana (Mar 2026). It is also an NRHA Pipeline Partner, has MEDITECH and Oracle standard integrations, and has a public "Become a Multiview Partner" form.
- ClearBalance went live at Livingston HealthCare (MT) on 2026-08-10. In 2026 it signed referral-style alliances with advisory firms (LJO Advisors, HARS/TORCH), which shows it partners this way.
- PayZen won 2026 Best in KLAS for patient financing and says it is "building a comprehensive AI platform" beyond financing. Watch for revenue cycle overlap.
- HMI sells denial appeals (physician advisors, RAC appeals), so it is high risk. HIA triages denials for appealability, so it is medium risk and a possible hand-off partner.
- Toyon's "Medicare and Medicaid Appeals" are cost report/reimbursement appeals, not claim-denial appeals. Rated medium.
- ArchPro bootcamps are funded by state Flex/SORH in 2026 (WA Apr 2026, WY 2025, MN, IL). This is a live education channel into CAH billing staff.
- SSI now markets "Real-Time Denial Insights" and denial prevention (thessigroup.com/denial-management-healthcare, 2026-05). It stays at medium risk.
