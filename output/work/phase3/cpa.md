## Key findings / surprises
- Eide Bailly audits Glendive Medical Center (GMC FAQ says "GMC currently contracts with Eide Bailey"), has a Billings MT office, audits Cabinet Peaks MC (MT) and prepares Livingston HealthCare's 990. DZA audits two MT CAHs (Missouri River MC, Anaconda). Wipfli has a Missoula partner.
- Stroudwater's Mahaska Health case recommended "a third-party vendor to focus on Medicare denials": a live example of consultants referring denials vendors.
- Wipfli job posting describes "outsourced revenue cycle engagements, including billing, collections, payer follow-up" with offshore staff: competitor risk could rise; verify.
- Forvis Mazars owns a "Denials Management Monitoring" analytics tool and ran a 2025 NRHA session on reducing rural denials; it is NRHA platinum sponsor.
- FTI + Penguin Ai (Dec 2025) remains the only CPA/advisory-firm AI appeals alliance found. No AI appeals alliance found at any rural CPA firm (search absence, not proof).
- Baker Tilly runs a formal technology alliances program and has a CAH case on Oracle Health CommunityWorks.
- Lutz (NE) proposal cites 50+ CAH clients and recommending denials management in RC reviews; Flex award not confirmed.
