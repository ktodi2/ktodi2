## 1. Rural-focused lenders, turnaround/management firms, and rural hospital operators/acquirers

- What it is / how hospitals buy through it:
  - Lenders: USDA Rural Development Community Facilities (CF) direct loans, guarantees and grants fund rural health care facilities, equipment and professional fees [https://www.rd.usda.gov/programs-services/community-facilities]. CF guaranteed loans: up to 80% guarantee, 1.25% up-front fee, population limit 50,000; direct loans for areas <=20,000 population [https://nosorh.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/102-Rural-Health-Capital-Resources-May-13-2025.pdf]. Commercial/Farm Credit lenders package CF loans (e.g., Compeer Financial led a $25.75M package incl. a $5M USDA CF guarantee for Midwest Medical Center, Galena IL) [https://www.compeer.com/press-releases/2025/april-2025/community-banks-meet-funding-challenges-for-rural-healthcare]. Stearns Bank markets USDA CF loan packaging services [https://www.stearnsbank.com/loans/usda-rural-development/loan-packaging-services]. Stroudwater Capital Partners offers USDA CF financing advisory [https://www.stroudwater.com/service/capital-planning-and-access/].
  - CoBank's Rural Infrastructure segment "serves ... rural health care and other community facilities" ($35.1B segment loans at 9/30/2025, but growth attributed to power/communications, not health care) [https://www.cobank.com/documents/7714906/134606537/CoBank-2025-Q3-Quarterly-Report.pdf/6eda6068-9f7f-24d8-245e-479929f3f290?t=1762468650158]. No evidence found of a CoBank vendor/partner program for hospitals.
  - USDA-funded TA: NRHA runs a USDA cooperative agreement (Rural Hospital Technical Assistance Program) that uses contractors for financial/operational assessments incl. "Revenue Cycle Analysis"; pilot $3M, initially 32 hospitals [https://www.ruralhealth.us/programs/center-for-rural-health-innovation-and-system-redesign/rural-hospital-technical-assistance-program] [https://nosorh.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/USDA-Update-Marcia-Comer.pdf]. Stroudwater did a USDA-funded, NRHA-facilitated CAH turnaround projecting a $7M revenue cycle improvement [https://www.stroudwater.com/case-study/a-strategic-financial-and-operational-transformation-for-a-western-cah/].
  - Turnaround / management companies: take interim CEO/CFO seats, so they directly choose RCM vendors. Healthcare Management Partners (Nashville) lists "Centralization of Business Offices" and "Revenue Cycle" in its turnaround scope and serves lenders, receiverships, Chapter 11s [https://www.hcmpllc.com/turnaround-management.html] [https://hcmpllc.com/about-us/]. HealthTech Management Services (formerly Brim Healthcare) is a rural hospital management company; its case study places interim CEO/CFO and implemented its own revenue cycle package after finding "little denial management" [https://www.health-tech.us/case-study-1/] [https://www.linkedin.com/in/joy-smith-9bb9b914 (per LinkedIn, seen 2026-09-24)]. Aletheia Health Partners (GA) offers hospital management plus its own RCM [https://valdostaceo.com/news/2025/07/aletheia-health-partners-beyond-cuts-how-rural-hospitals-are-finding-growth-through-strategic-partnerships/]. Kerith Brook Health offers rural hospital management incl. revenue cycle support [https://www.kerithbrookhealth.com/services-5-1]. Independent interim CFO Jim Basch lists denials management and "Vendor optimization (Waystar, Medhost, etc.)" [https://jimbasch.com/].
  - Operators/acquirers: BradenHealth (TN, VA) has "assumed operations of dozens of hospitals since 2020" and brings "processes back in house" [https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/hospital-management-administration/how-bradenhealth-puts-its-money-where-its-mouth-is-to-reopen-rural-hospitals/] (May 2025). Rural Healthcare Group (Kinderhook-backed) is a rural primary care platform (clinics, not hospitals) that acquired Steward Medical Group [https://bassberry.com/experience/acquisition-of-a-provider-and-network-management-organization/]. American Healthcare Systems (Sarian) owns Randolph Health (NC) and hospitals in IL/TX, with 2026 litigation alleging fund siphoning and unpaid vendors [https://www.theassemblync.com/news/health/healthcare/mike-sarian-randolph-hospital-lawsuits/] (June 2026). Lifepoint made R1 its exclusive RCM partner for >1/3 of its hospitals (2020) [https://www.r1rcm.com/newsroom/lifepoint-health-and-r1-rcm-announce-partnership-to-deliver-revenue-cycle-excellence-and-enhance-patient-experience]. CHS 2026 divestitures went to nonprofit systems (Huntsville Hospital, Freeman, VUMC), not rural roll-ups [https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260401712521/en/Community-Health-Systems-Completes-Divestiture-of-Huntsville-Alabama-Hospital-to-Huntsville-Hospital-Health-System] [https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260202944629/en/Community-Health-Systems-Completes-Sale-of-Ownership-Interests-in-Clarksville-Tennessee-Hospital-to-Vanderbilt-University-Medical-Center].
- Typical economics: USDA CF guarantee fee 1.25% + 0.50% annual renewal (a cost to hospitals, not a partner fee) [https://nosorh.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/USDA-Update-Marcia-Comer.pdf]. Turnaround/management firms bill fixed fee/contingency (Jim Basch: "Fixed fee, contingency") [https://jimbasch.com/]. No published referral-fee arrangements found for lenders or turnaround firms: NOT_FOUND.
- Realistic reach: HMP ~24 employees per LinkedIn (seen 2026-09-24) [https://linkedin.com/company/healthcare-management-partners-llc]; engagements are few and distressed. BradenHealth: "dozens" of hospitals (TN, VA). USDA CF touches many CAHs, but only on capital projects. (judgment) Each org reaches few hospitals, but a turnaround firm's interim CFO decides quickly.
- Speed to first deal: (judgment) Fast (1-3 months) for an interim CFO/turnaround firm that is already running a distressed CAH with a denials backlog; slow or unlikely for lenders (CoBank/USDA do not steer vendor choice).
- Competitor risk: HIGH for management companies that run their own RCM: Ovation/Amplify (known), HealthTech (own revenue cycle package, plus "Impekkable Technology" revenue recovery via Western Healthcare Alliance) [https://www.wha1.org/healthtech/], Aletheia (own RCM), Curi (see section 2). MEDIUM for HMP (lists "Revenue Cycle" and "Centralization of Business Offices" as turnaround workstreams, not a product). LOW for lenders. For operators: HIGH where RCM is centralized with an outsourcer (Lifepoint/R1); (judgment) small operators like BradenHealth insource and may be buyers, not channels.
- Partner motions: refer (turnaround firms, interim CFOs, Stroudwater-type consultants); fund (USDA TA via NRHA contractor slot); lenders: low fit.
- Example orgs: see Candidate table.
- Taken categories: Lifepoint -> R1 [https://www.r1rcm.com/newsroom/lifepoint-health-and-r1-rcm-announce-partnership-to-deliver-revenue-cycle-excellence-and-enhance-patient-experience]; Ovation/QHR -> Amplify (formerly ResolutionRCM, "110 independent hospitals", includes denials management) [https://teamiha.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/ResolutionRCM_OnePager_2022.pdf] [https://pestakeholder.org/news/ovation-healthcare-finds-profit-in-nonprofit-rural-health-providers/]; HealthTech/WHA -> Impekkable Technology [https://www.wha1.org/healthtech/].
- Vetting criteria: NOT_FOUND for this type.
- Notes / surprises: (1) American Healthcare Systems is in active fraud litigation (2026); avoid. (2) CAH turnarounds routinely cite revenue cycle as the biggest lever (Stroudwater $7M projection), so turnaround consultants are natural referrers. (3) Jackson Parish Hospital (LA CAH) returned to TruBridge CBO in 2025 [https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250804655921/en/TruBridge-Re-Establishes-Partnership-With-Jackson-Parish-Hospital-to-Boost-Financial-Performance-and-Outcomes]; TruBridge is being acquired by IKS (announced Apr 23, 2026) per same Business Wire feed.

## 2. Malpractice insurers, captives, mutuals and hospital-owned insurance networks

- What it is: MPL carriers insure hospitals and physicians and add risk-management "value-added services". Some physician-facing carriers run preferred-vendor discount programs, but these target medical practices, not hospital revenue cycle.
  - Integris Group "Member Advantage+" preferred vendors (Payment Pros, TSI collections, AMR recruiting); Integris states it "does not receive a fee or other compensation from the preferred vendors" [https://www.integrisgrp.com/Member-Advantage].
  - CAP (California) "CAPAdvantage" discounted practice-management programs from vetted vendors [https://www.capphysicians.com/physicians-and-surgeons-coverage/benefits].
  - LAMMICO's agency (Elatas Risk Partners) offers a credit card processing program via PSFS [https://www.lammico.com/why-lammico].
  - MedPro "Advantage Program for Hospitals" = bundled cyber/regulatory coverage, not vendor discounts [https://resource.medpro.com/hospitals-advantage-program]. Coverys: >500 hospitals, health centers and clinics insured; RiskRx PRO, Med-IQ education [https://www.duckcreek.com/customer/coverys/] [https://www.coverys.com/insurance-offerings/organizations/hospitals-systems/medical-professional-liability-insurance].
  - Constellation (MMIC, UMIA, Arkansas Mutual) merged with Curi (announced Feb 2023) [https://curi.com/press-releases/curi-constellation-announce-plans-to-merge/]. MMIC historically served Upper Midwest clinics and hospitals (IA, MN, NE, ND, SD, WI, KS, MO) [https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/midwest/2010/09/21/113410.htm] (2010, stale). No Constellation/Curi "vendor partner" discount program for hospitals found: NOT_FOUND.
  - COMPETITOR: Curi Advisory launched "Revenue Cycle Solutions" on 2026-02-19 for hospitals and physician practices (insurance A/R resolution, AI automation, payer intelligence), led by MD Chad Peters [https://curi.com/press-releases/curi-advisory-revenue-cycle-solutions/] [https://curi.com/advisory/managed-solutions/].
  - Hospital-owned captives/networks: Kentucky Hospital Insurance Company RRG, owned by member hospitals, managed by KHA Solutions Group, serves KY and TN [https://www.kyha.com/kha-solutions-group/kentucky-hospital-insurance-company/]. Yellowstone Insurance Exchange RRG, member-owned PL/GL, via NM Hospital Services Corporation [https://www.nmhospitalservices.com/yellowstone]. KHA Workers' Compensation Fund (Kansas Health Service Corporation), >70 members [https://kha-net.org/aboutkha/khsc/khaworkerscompensationfund/].
  - **Montana Health Network (MHN), Miles City MT**: hospital-owned; provides GL/PL malpractice, workers' comp trust ("largest self-insured in the State of Montana"), employee health plan, staffing, education, consulting, and credentialing via Big Sky Credentialing [https://montanahealthnetwork.com/insurance] [https://montanahealthnetwork.com/business-services]. Shareholders include **Glendive Medical Center** (Aegis's customer), Billings Clinic, Beartooth Billings Clinic, Central Montana Medical Center, Dahl Memorial, Daniels Memorial, Fallon Medical Complex, Frances Mahon Deaconess, Holy Rosary, McCone County Health Center, Northeast Montana Health Services, Phillips County Hospital, Roosevelt Medical Center, Sheridan Memorial, Sidney Health Center, St. John's United, Wheatland Memorial [https://montanahealthnetwork.com/about-us]. Serves "17 owner healthcare organizations and approximately 40 other healthcare entities" [https://www.ruralhealthinfo.org/rural-monitor/montana-health-network] (2023). CEO Chris Hopkins; CFO Lori Backes; VP Strategy Ward VanWichen [https://montanahealthnetwork.com/about-us].
- Typical economics: Insurer preferred-vendor programs are usually member discounts with no fee to the insurer (Integris explicitly no fee) [https://www.integrisgrp.com/Member-Advantage]. MPL premium credits of 2.5% to 10% are used to reward risk activities (e.g., ProAssurance EHR credit 2.5%) [https://agents.proassurance.com/hubfs/Agents%20Website/Marketing%20Materials/Regional%20and%20State%20Specific/M3678_15%20WMSMemberBenefitCondensedPacket_D1Final.pdf?hsLang=en]. No MPL carrier found paying or receiving RCM referral fees: NOT_FOUND.
- Reach: Coverys >500 facilities; MedPro >300,000 insureds [https://www.medpro.com/get-coverage]; MHN 17 owners + ~40 entities in MT.
- Speed: (judgment) Slow for national carriers (denials are not a liability-risk topic; they sell risk management). Fast for MHN because Glendive is an owner and MHN already runs shared business services for eastern MT CAHs.
- Competitor risk: HIGH for Curi (own RCM service since Feb 2026). LOW for Coverys, MedPro, TDC, ProAssurance (no RCM product found). LOW for MHN (no RCM product found; offers credentialing/payer enrollment via Big Sky Credentialing).
- Partner motions: endorse / refer (MHN: shared-service add-on or member offer); national MPL carriers: low fit (possible content/webinar co-marketing only, judgment).
- Taken categories: Curi Advisory RCM (own). MHN: NOT_FOUND.
- Vetting criteria: NOT_FOUND for hospital-facing insurer vendor programs.
- Notes / surprises: MHN is the single most relevant find in this section: a Montana, hospital-owned shared-services company whose owners include Glendive Medical Center and ~16 other MT hospitals, most of them CAHs. (judgment) Warm path via Glendive leadership.
