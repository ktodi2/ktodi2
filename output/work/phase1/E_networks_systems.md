## Section 1: Rural health networks, CAH networks, co-ops and rural CINs

### What it is / how hospitals buy through it
- These are hospital-owned or hospital-governed collaboratives of independent rural hospitals. Common forms are 501(c)(3) networks, member-owned LLCs and co-ops, and clinically integrated networks (CINs). They run peer groups (CFO, revenue cycle, CEO), negotiate vendor contracts, and some run shared services. Members usually opt in to each contract individually. Examples: Medi-Sota says "no contracts or services are mandatory" (https://medi-sota.org/main/index.php/en/membership-benefits). Cibolo networks say "hospital members can choose which of these collaborations to participate in" (https://hcinnovationgroup.com/finance-revenue-cycle/hospital-and-health-system-operations/news/55295134/nebraska-adds-cibolos-fifth-rural-clinically-integrated-network).
- Vendor vetting sometimes runs through a formal process:
  - Western Healthcare Alliance (WHA) says its corporate partners are "carefully vetted by staff and members". WHA then manages the master agreement and gets quarterly savings reports (https://wha1.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/WHA-Hospitals-and-Large-Healthcare-Orgs-Web-2-23-26.pdf).
  - In 2014, WHA picked a centralized business office (CBO) partner after "a ten-month due diligence process" (stale, 2014: https://prweb.com/releases/western_healthcare_alliance_partners_with_avec_health_solutions_to_expand_centralized_business_office_solutions_for_rural_and_community_healthcare_providers/prweb11953248.htm).
  - The Rural Collaborative's enterprise arm (Rural Health Enterprise) used a formal RFP in March 2026 (details below).
- The National Cooperative of Health Networks Association (NCHN) is the national association of these networks. It has about 40 network members and runs a paid Business Partner program with an annual conference (https://nchn.org/ajax/get-state-info.html?state= ; https://nchn.org/site/business-partner-prospectus).

### Typical economics
- **Revenue share and admin fees exist.**
  - The Rural Health Enterprise (RHE) RFP requires the selected RCM vendor to sign a master services agreement that "recognizes the value of the administrative support provided by RHE", "inclusive of program support administration fee". It also requires group purchasing discounts tied to membership growth (https://ruralcollaborative.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Request-For-Proposal-Hospital-and-Clinic-Billing-Services-Final-031926.pdf).
  - The Illinois Critical Access Hospital Network (ICAHN) says it "may receive important financial support as services are utilized by our members" (stale, 2021 annual report: https://icahn.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/2021-ICAHN-Annual-Report.pdf).
  - The Indiana Statewide Rural Health Network (InSRHN) has "a small portion of revenue coming from a revenue stream from shared savings" (https://ruralhealthinfo.org/project-examples/637).
  - Utah's Rural 9 network shares savings with members (https://ruralhealthinfo.org/project-examples/745).
- **Member dues are small.** Utah Rural 9 charges $1,000/month (source above). Medi-Sota charges $5,500/yr (https://medi-sota.org/main/index.php/en/membership-benefits). InSRHN charges $5,000/yr (per an undated slideshow copy: https://slideserve.com/kenaz/insrhn-membership-costs-and-benefits-powerpoint-ppt-presentation).
- **Cibolo networks** "pay an annual fee to Cibolo Health, a for-profit company, for management services" (KFF Health News, 2025-09-09: https://kffhealthnews.org/rural-health/rural-hospitals-north-dakota-cins-rough-rider-network-closures).
- **Grant money flows through networks.**
  - Washington's Rural Health Transformation Program (RHTP) application proposes $5-6M/yr for The Rural Collaborative (TRC), covering revenue cycle capacity, AI tools and shared billing/coding staff. It also proposes a $32-42M/yr infrastructure fund (AI, RCM, cyber) administered by the Washington State Hospital Association (WSHA) (https://hca.wa.gov/assets/program/rhtp-project-narrative.pdf).
  - North Dakota gave $3.5M to launch the Rough Rider Network (KFF, above).
  - The Arkansas Rural Health Partnership (ARHP) reports more than $72M in grants obtained (https://arruralhealth.org/who-we-are/leaders).
  - Cibolo's EVP says "several of our networks were mentioned" by CMS as a good use of RHTP funds (HCI, 2026-07-13: https://hcinnovationgroup.com/finance-revenue-cycle/hospital-and-health-system-operations/article/55390476/why-kansas-joined-6-other-states-in-creating-high-value-networks).
- **Aegis model fit (judgment).** A network admin fee or revenue share on top of Aegis's existing 15% first-year referral fee is the norm. RHTP sub-awards are a realistic "fund" motion in Washington, and likely in other states where networks are named in RHTP plans.

### Realistic reach (numbers, sourced)
- **NCHN:** about 40 network members plus associates (roster above). Many members are not hospital networks (for example CHW or women's health networks).
- **Cibolo "high value networks":** 7 as of June 2026.
  - Counts on cibolohealth.com/networks (undated, seen 2026-09-24): ND 23, MN 17, MT 24, OH 24, WI 10, NE 19, plus KS 7 at its 2026-06-17 launch. That sums to about 124 hospitals (INFERENCE: sum of listed counts).
  - Nate White said Cibolo-affiliated networks "will represent more than 120 hospitals" (KFF, 2025-09-09).
  - Sources: https://cibolohealth.com/networks ; https://prweb.com/releases/seven-independent-rural-hospitals-launch-kansas-high-value-network-302803261.html
- **Show-Me High Value Network (MO):** 23 hospitals at its September 2026 launch (https://mohospitals.org/newsroom/independent-missouri-hospitals-unite-to-launch-show-me-high-value-network). The Missouri Hospital Association (MHA) release does not name a manager (Cibolo involvement NOT_FOUND).
- **Largest single networks:** Heartland Health Alliance (Bryan Health) 59; Prairie Health Ventures 50+ ("59 hospitals/owners" on the NCHN page); ICAHN 60 organizations; Medi-Sota 37; The Rural Collaborative (TRC) 31; WHA 31 (28 independent).

### Speed to first deal (judgment)
- **Fast, 1-3 months:** peer-group sponsorship and education slots. Many networks run revenue cycle roundtables that invite outside experts: InSRHN, Utah Rural 9, Wilderness Health, NCHN webinars.
- **Medium, 3-6 months:** preferred-vendor listing with a revenue share (ICAHN, WHA, Medi-Sota, PHV). Each still needs one-by-one hospital sales.
- **Slow, 6-12+ months:** network-wide master agreements, RFP cycles and RHTP-funded purchases. Examples: the RHE RFP, and Cibolo networks' business integration committees.
- The Montana networks can move faster for Aegis because Glendive Medical Center is a member of both Montana Health Network and Yellowstone HVN, which gives a peer reference inside each.

### Competitor risk
- **Medium overall, and varies by network.**
- **High where the network or its manager sells RCM or denials services:**
  - **Cibolo Health.** Its services "include medical billing, coding, accounts receivable management, and denial management". It made TruBridge the preferred RCM partner for its CIN members (BusinessWire, 2025-02-12: https://businesswire.com/news/home/20250212546655/en/TruBridge-Selected-by-Cibolo-Health-as-a-Preferred-Partner-for-RCM-Technology-and-Services).
  - **Great Plains Health Alliance (GPHA).** Runs a CBO with Oracle Health, paid as a percentage of collections (https://gpha.com/services).
  - **WHA's sister company Healthcare Management** (A-1 Collection Agency and AR Services). This is an extended business office for early-out self-pay, not denials.
  - **ICAHN** does coding in-house and lists OS inc. (billing and denial management) as a preferred partner.
  - **New England Collaborative Health Network (NECHN).** "Ovation Healthcare is the engine within" it (https://nechnetwork.com/who-we-are). Ovation's Amplify RCM is a known competitor.
- **Low where the network is peer-group, GPO or insurance only:** Montana Health Network, Northwest Hospital Alliance, Utah Rural 9, the New Mexico Rural Hospital Network (NMRHN), InSRHN and the Upper Peninsula Health Care Solutions (UPHCS) network.

### Partner motions that apply
- refer, endorse (preferred vendor), contract (network master agreement), fund (RHTP and HRSA sub-awards)
- Integrate only indirectly, through each network's common EHR mix.

   - Launched 2025-05-15 with 24 Montana CAHs. The member list includes **Glendive Medical Center** (https://yellowstonenetwork.org/networks).
3. **Montana Health Network (Miles City)**, https://montanahealthnetwork.com
   - Owned by 17 facilities, **including Glendive Medical Center and Billings Clinic**. Serves "approximately 40 other healthcare entities" (RHIhub, 2023-05-10).
   - Services are insurance trusts, staffing and education. No RCM service seen.
4. **Western Healthcare Alliance (CO/UT/MI Thumb)**, https://wha1.org
   - 31 members (21 CAHs). Has a revenue-cycle sister company. Uses a formal vendor-vetting process.

- **Caret Health** (care-gap outreach, not RCM): NCHN partner and Rough Rider pilot.

### Vetting criteria / what networks require from vendors
- **RHE RFP requirements:**
  - CAH/RHC experience, the number of CAH clients in the last 2 years, 3 CAH references, and Washington experience.
  - The vendor must state "if all claims and denial tasks are handled directly within the host EHR or within a proprietary system" and describe "use of AI or automation in ... denials management".
  - KPI-based incentives and penalties, a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement (BAA), an RHE master services agreement with an admin fee, and tiered pricing.
- **WHA:** vetting by staff and members, quarterly savings reporting, and volume-based discounts.
- **Medi-Sota:** a vendor application path ("become a Medi-Sota Contract Vendor").

### Notes / surprises
- **Glendive Medical Center sits in three channels at once.** It owns part of Montana Health Network, it is a Yellowstone HVN member (Cibolo), and it is a Billings Clinic management affiliate on Oracle Health. These are three warm paths to Montana peers.
- **Cibolo is the fastest-growing rural CIN operator** (7 states, about 124 hospitals, 10 states in progress per its EVP). It is also an RCM competitor and already allied with TruBridge. Judgment: approach network boards and CFO committees, not Cibolo corporate, or position Aegis as a denials-only tool that sits alongside TruBridge.
- **The Rural Collaborative is the strongest near-term lead in this category.** Denials pain is documented (Molina denial dashboard in its 2024 annual report), RHTP money is flowing, 15 of 31 members run MEDITECH or Oracle, and an RCM RFP was issued in 2026 (award status NOT_FOUND).

## Section 2: Health systems with rural affiliate, management or outreach programs

### What it is / how hospitals buy through it
- Regional systems sign management agreements with independent CAHs. Under these, the system often employs the CEO and provides consulting and shared services for a fee. Some systems also host their EHR for affiliates (Epic Community Connect, or Oracle/Cerner for Billings Clinic and GPHA).
- The fee level is shown in two examples:
  - Avera's management fee was "1.5 percent of budgeted expenses" at Pipestone (stale, 2010: https://pipestonestar.com/articles/presentation-covers-avera-pcmc-relationship).
  - Ochsner's management deal with its LSU hospitals charged 3% of revenue, and billing was separate (2018: https://jlcb.legis.la.gov/Docs/2018/sep/09-18-18%20Agenda%20%236%20-%20Management%20Services%20Agreement.pdf).
- The system's regional CFO or "network financial executive" often oversees the affiliates' business offices. This is the decision point for Aegis. Example: the Billings Clinic Network CFO role covers "charge entry and patient billing related to revenue cycle and management of account receivables" (undated job post: https://clients-eidebailly.icims.com/jobs/7488/billings-clinic---network-financial-executive-cfo/job?in_iframe=1).

### Typical economics
- Management fees are paid by the affiliate to the system. There is no public evidence of systems taking vendor referral fees (NOT_FOUND).
- The realistic motion is "resell/bundle": the system adds Aegis to its affiliate services menu, or it becomes a customer across its managed hospitals (judgment).
- Epic Community Connect is priced on a cost-recovery basis. UI Health Care: "We don't run it in the red, but we're not doing it to make a margin" (https://beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ehrs/big-success-ui-health-care-connects-9-hospitals-to-epic).

### Realistic reach (sourced)
- **Billings Clinic-Logan Health:** 16 regional CAH/clinic partnerships (https://billingsclinic.com/about-us).
- **Avera:** about 20 independent partner hospitals on its MyChart/EHR extension (https://avera.org/mychart/technical-support). Its management network was described as "Avera McKennan's 14 member rural hospital network" (per LinkedIn profile of Curt Hohman, seen 2026-09).
- **Intermountain:** "about 40 smaller rural hospitals", mainly telehealth (MedCity 2025-02-10).
- **Great Plains Health Alliance (GPHA):** "over 30" hospitals.
- **UnityPoint:** a management services agreement (MSA) model "similar to ... 18 other hospitals" (stale, 2019).
- **UI Health Care Community Connect Iowa:** 9 hospital systems plus 5 on the waitlist.
- **Heartland Health Alliance (Bryan):** 59.
- **MercyOne North Iowa:** 6 managed (2023).
- **Great Plains Health (North Platte):** outreach to 26 CAHs.
- **Epic Community Connect** captured "nearly 70% of all hospitals with EHR decisions among smaller hospitals in 2024" (KLAS via Becker's 2026-06-11: https://beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ehrs/epic-community-connect-explained-what-small-hospitals-get).

### Speed to first deal (judgment)
- **Slow, 6-12 months.** System procurement, IT security review and integration with the host EHR are required. A system's Epic Community Connect affiliates can only add tools the host approves.
- **Faster where the system uses Oracle Health or MEDITECH and manages affiliates' finance centrally.** Billings Clinic-Logan Health and GPHA are the prime examples.

### Competitor risk
- **High** where the system or manager runs affiliates' billing:
  - **GPHA:** CBO with Oracle Health, "percentage of monthly collections" (https://gpha.com/services).
  - **Ochsner:** does facility billing and collection under management agreements (2018 MSA above).
- **Medium** where the system's regional finance team "consults" on the business office:
  - **Billings Clinic-Logan Health.**
  - **Avera.** It has a VP of Patient Financial Services who also holds a "Billing Services Officer" title (per LinkedIn, seen 2026-09). Whether Avera bills for affiliates is NOT_FOUND.
- **Low** for clinical, telehealth or consulting-only programs: Bryan Heartland, Intermountain, Great Plains Health, Hendrick.

### Partner motions that apply
- resell/bundle (system services menu), refer (regional CFO to affiliates), integrate (EHR host: Oracle Health at Billings Clinic and GPHA, Epic Community Connect hosts)

### Existing RCM vendors embedded
- TruBridge; Oracle Health (GPHA CBO)
- Optum: Bassett outsourced its RCM and IT (https://medcitynews.com/2021/05/bassett-healthcare-network-outsources-it-rcm-operations-to-optum)
- Availity Essentials Pro: Sanford clearinghouse/RCM (2024-12-10: https://availity.com/news/sanford-health-partners-with-availity-for-revenue-cycle-management)
- Multiview ERP: GPHA preferred partner (2024-09-05)

### Notes / surprises
- **Billings Clinic-Logan Health is the best system target (judgment).**
  - It runs Oracle Health, which Aegis supports, and is extending Oracle to all 30 hospitals, replacing MEDITECH at Logan sites (Oracle PR 2024-10-23).
  - Glendive Medical Center is a Billings Clinic management affiliate (2013 agreement: Billings Clinic "will assist with recruiting and employ the Chief Executive Officer").
  - Other affiliates: Beartooth, Big Horn, Central Montana MC, Colstrip, Daniels, Livingston, Pioneer, Roundup, Sheridan, Stillwater, Wheatland, plus 5 in Wyoming (https://billingsclinic.com/about-us/affiliate-hospitals-clinics).
- **Epic Community Connect is spreading fast and pulls affiliates off MEDITECH and CPSI** (examples: Sidney Health Center moved MEDITECH to Epic via Sanford in 2014; Veterans Memorial moved CPSI to Epic via UI Health Care).
  - This shrinks Aegis's addressable EHR base inside system-affiliated hospitals, unless Aegis supports Epic.
  - Avera itself is transitioning to Epic (LinkedIn post 2025-07-08).
  - Aspen Valley Health (a CAH) launched a CAH-hosted Epic Connect network with Heart of the Rockies (https://aspenvalleyhealth.org/news/aspen-valley-health-launches-epic-connect-partnership-pioneering-new-collaboration-model-for-rural-hospitals).
- **Systems not researched in depth, or with no rural back-office program found:** Mayo, Allina, Geisinger, Marshfield (now merged with Essentia), CommonSpirit/CHI (it exited PHV ownership), Monument Health (5 own hospitals; affiliate program NOT_FOUND), St. Luke's Idaho (Evolent value-based partnership only, stale 2016), Baptist Health KY (joint venture at Madisonville), Logan Health (merged into Billings Clinic).
