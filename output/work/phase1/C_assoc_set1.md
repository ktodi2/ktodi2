# C. State hospital association service arms (set 1)
Status: COMPLETE (set 1: 21 states). Researched 2026-09-24.

## State hospital association service arms (set 1)

### Type-level summary (template fields)
- What it is / how hospitals buy through it: Most state hospital associations own a for-profit (or shared-services) subsidiary that vets vendors, signs a contract (often with pre-negotiated member pricing/terms), and markets the vendor to members with an endorsement letter, mailings, events and warm introductions; hospitals still buy directly from the vendor. Examples: THA Innovative Solutions (TN) https://thainnovativesolutions.com/process/ ; MHA Solutions (MS), which is treated as a GPO under Mississippi public-purchasing rules https://www.mhanet.org/Online/Solutions/Prospective%20Endorsed%20Partner.aspx ; LHA Solutions (LA) https://lhasolutions.org/LHA%20Solutions/LHASolutions/Main/VendorInformation.aspx
- Typical economics (public figures only): TN $25,000/yr plus revenue share, category exclusivity https://thainnovativesolutions.com/become-a-partner/ ; NM HSC minimum monthly marketing fee or negotiated % of fees https://docest.com/doc/595811/hospital-services-corporation ; OK revenue share + $25,000 Platinum Underwriter tier; 16 partners, >40 hospitals, ~$180k/yr non-dues revenue (2023) https://www.okoha.com/Common/Uploaded%20files/ohadocs/publications%20and%20resources/annual%20reports/2023%20ANNUAL%20REPORT.pdf ; SC revenue share, Solvent Networks ~50% of SCHA budget (2019) https://solventnetworks.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Solvent_PartnerDirectory_Digital_19.pdf ; VA invitation-only, "often with revenue-sharing" https://vhhasolutions.com/become-a-partner/ ; TX associate corporate membership $5,000/yr https://www.tha.org/services-for-hospitals/partners/associate-corporate-membership/ ; TN hospitals using partners get a dues credit https://thainnovativesolutions.com/members/
- Realistic reach into rural/CAH/community hospitals (sourced counts): TORCH 157 rural TX hospitals; OK 90 rural incl. 39 CAHs; KY 29 CAHs/72 rural of 126; AL 52 rural; MS 73 rural (MHA dataset); NV NRHP 13 CAHs; AR 105 members (~29 CAHs, 2015); TN 160+ members; GA 145-170; VA 117; NC 130-150; SC ~100; NM 47-48; OR 59 (32 small/rural); UT 63; LA 150+; OH 252; TX THA 450+; FL 200+. Sources in state sections. Rough set-1 total: ~2,300 member hospitals, several hundred rural/CAH (INFERENCE: not deduplicated).
- Speed to first deal (judgment): Endorsement itself takes 3 to 9 months where published (TN: quarterly board, 2% acceptance) https://thainnovativesolutions.com/process/ ; KY adds hospital "Shark Tank" sessions https://www.kyha.com/kentucky-hospital-association-partners-with-esp-global/ . First hospital deal after endorsement: likely 1-2 quarters given warm intros. Faster paths: rural-focused service arms that already sell RCM tools (TORCH/TMSI, OK, AR) and sponsor/speaking slots at RCM roundtables (MS) or managed care conferences (FL).
- Competitor risk (by channel): HIGH where denials/RCM outsourcers already endorsed: MS (Trilogy RCM exclusive; CorroHealth), KY (CorroHealth; FairCode appeal letters), TX-TORCH (TruBridge, Meduit denials mgmt, SlicedHealth), OK (Coronis). MED: VA (Premier partner; AI appetite), OH (AblePay, Util Auditors; open directory), LA (Infinx sponsor). LOW/open slot: TN, AR, NC, SC (PARA gone), WV, AL, GA, THA-Texas, NM. Sources in state sections.
- Partner motions that apply: endorse (primary); refer (associations' member-ambassador teams, e.g., THA TX "pipeline calls"); contract (MS MHA Solutions GPO status for public hospitals); integrate (NM HSC HealthXnet clearinghouse); fund (GA GREAT Health ARCHER $45M tech fund; FL RHTP $209M; state RHTP programs).
- Example orgs with one rural fact each: TORCH/TMSI (157 rural TX hospitals; TMSI board of rural CEOs) https://texlatrc.org/2026-digital-health-summit/meet-the-speakers/ ; OHA Health Services (39 CAHs among 90 rural OK hospitals) https://www.okoha.com/OHA/OHA/Health_Care_Issues/Rural_Health/Rural_Health.aspx ; NRHP (13 CAHs, RCM Improvement Program incl. denials management) https://nrhp.org/what-we-do/revenue-cycle-improvement-program/ ; WVHS/WVHA (runs CAH Network) https://wvha.org/about-wvha/wvha-team/
- Existing RCM / denials vendors embedded (taken categories): AblePay (TX, OH, KY, OK, AR, NC, VA); CorroHealth/PARA (KY, MS; SC 2022); FairCode appeal letters (KY); Trilogy RCM + PayZen (MS); TruBridge, Meduit, SlicedHealth (TORCH); Coronis (OK); Callagy Recovery NSA IDR, HYVE payer scorecard (AR); RetroCheck, Qualify Health (NC); Turquoise, Xsolis (TN); ARxChange, ModusOne (GA); CommerceHealthcare (LA, NC, VA, WV). Sponsors (not endorsed): Infinx (LA), TruBridge (AL), R1/Savista/Premier Medical Appeals/MEDHOST (TORCH corporate members).
- Vetting criteria (sourced): TN 5-step with hospital leader evaluation and board of CEOs/CFOs/COOs https://thainnovativesolutions.com/process/ ; KY KHASG Board review + Shark Tank; TX RFI (product summary, pitch, industry partners, goals) https://www.tha.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/THA-Member-Solutions-Endorsed-Partner-RFI_2025.pdf ; NM asks for other association endorsements and NM/TX/CO/AZ client lists https://docest.com/doc/595811/hospital-services-corporation ; MS info used in "final approval presentation to the MHA Solutions Board" https://www.mhanet.org/Online/Solutions/Prospective%20Endorsed%20Partner.aspx ; WV intake form asks for current GPO/association relationships.
- Notes / surprises: (1) AzHHA's Affiliated Partners Program is suspended (2026). (2) Mississippi: many hospitals left MHA in 2023 for the Mississippi Healthcare Collaborative (525+ orgs), so MHA reach is reduced; MHC should be checked. (3) KY already endorses a clinical appeal-letter writer (FairCode) for DRG downgrades. (4) VHHA Solutions endorsed an agentic-AI platform (Andor) in Feb 2026, showing openness to AI endorsements. (5) SCHA created Qualivis, which is now endorsed by 27 associations: a model for a vendor scaling state-by-state through association endorsements. (6) MEDITECH is a TORCH corporate member and an LHA Silver Sponsor (2026). (7) Several associations publish payer-denial data (OHA, AlaHA collaborative, AzHHA annual report), giving Aegis an education/advocacy entry point.
