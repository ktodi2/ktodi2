### Texas: TORCH (Texas Organization of Rural & Community Hospitals) / TORCH Management Services, Inc. (TMSI)
- Service arm: TMSI, "wholly owned subsidiary of TORCH"; runs "Endorsed Partners" = "specially selected group of companies whose experience working with rural hospitals has undergone a thorough verification process"; endorsement approved by TMSI Board. https://www.torchnet.org/uploads/1/1/9/5/119501126/kpn_press_release-webebp.pdf (2021)
- Two tiers: Endorsed Partners https://www.torchnet.org/endorsed-partners.html and Corporate Members (by service line) https://www.torchnet.org/corporate-members.html ; annual "Endorsed Partner of the Year" award (TruBridge 2024, SlicedHealth 2025). https://trubridge.com/resources/trubridge-awarded-torch-management-services-2024-endorsed-partner-of-the-year/ ; https://slicedhealth.com/post/slicedhealth-awarded-torch-management-services-inc-endorsed-partner-of-the-year-for-2025/
- TMSI board is made of rural hospital CEOs/CFOs. https://www.torchnet.org/boardofdirectorstmsi.html
- Members: "157 rural Texas Hospitals" (TEXLA 2026 summit bio) https://texlatrc.org/2026-digital-health-summit/meet-the-speakers/ ; "161 rural Texas Hospitals" (TAMU bio, undated) https://public-health.tamu.edu/dean/board/john-henderson.html
- Taken categories (endorsed): TruBridge (RCM, coding, collections; endorsed since 2014); Meduit (EBO, early out, bad debt, "Denials Management"); SlicedHealth (AI contract modeling, "underpayments and denials", claim estimation); DoorSpace (credentialing/enrollment); Forvis Mazars, Durbin & Co (reimbursement/cost report); Alliant Purchasing (Premier GPO). https://www.torchnet.org/endorsed-partners.html
- Corporate members in RCM (not endorsed, just members): Premier Medical Appeals (Houston), R1, Savista, MEDHOST, MediCommerce, RevSpring, PatientPay, 835 Recovery, EqualizeRCM, Allyhealth, Medsphere, OnQ Global, The Rural Advantage; MEDITECH and Oracle Health are corporate members. https://www.torchnet.org/corporate-members.html ; https://web.torchnet.org/corporate/Revenue-Cycle-Management,-Collections
- AI denial tool endorsed? SlicedHealth (AI underpayment/denial analytics) is endorsed; no appeal-writing AI seen.
- People (official staff page https://www.torchnet.org/our-team.html): John Henderson, CEO/President; Russell Bishop, President, TMSI (listed "Vice-President, TMSI" on TMSI board page https://www.torchnet.org/boardofdirectorstmsi.html); Megan Cody, Director of Marketing, TMSI.
