## 1. CPA / advisory firms serving CAHs and community hospitals (set 2)

- **What it is / how hospitals buy through it:** Regional and national CPA firms audit hospital financial statements, prepare Medicare/Medicaid cost reports, and sell reimbursement and revenue-cycle consulting. Several now sell revenue-cycle assessments that explicitly include denial management (e.g., LBMC "front, middle and back ... denial management" https://www.lbmc.com/services/healthcare-consulting/revenue-cycle-management/ ; PYA lists "Denials and Appeals Management" https://www.pyapc.com/healthcare-managed-care-reimbursement/ ; Brown Edwards lists "revenue cycle management, cost reporting" https://becpas.com/industries/healthcare/). Hospitals buy by annual engagement letter (audit, cost report) and follow-on consulting SOWs. The auditor/cost-report preparer relationship recurs every year with the CFO (judgment).
- **Typical economics:** No published referral fees for CPA firms found (NOT_FOUND). Independence rules limit what an auditor can resell to an attest client (judgment; the Baker Tilly release itself describes an "alternative practice structure in accordance with the AICPA Code" separating attest from advisory https://www.bakertilly.com/news/baker-tilly-and-moss-adams-to-combine). Advisory arms do run technology alliances: Baker Tilly lists alliances incl. Definitive Healthcare and MediSpend https://www.bakertilly.com/services/technology-alliances . Comparable consultant referral programs from RCM/AI vendors: SunKnowledge pays 10% of gross monthly revenue for account lifetime https://sunknowledge.com/rcm-consulting/ ; Revenue Synergy pays 10% of first-year collections https://www.revenuesynergy.com/partners.html ; Aerolib AI (AI appeals) runs referral/reseller/strategic tiers for consultants https://aerolib.ai/channel-partner .
- **Realistic reach (numbers):**
  - Plante Moran: reimbursement team "completes more than 1,200 cost reports each year" (all provider types) https://www.plantemoran.com/explore-our-thinking/areas-of-focus/reimbursement-and-payment-advisory-services
  - Carr, Riggs & Ingram: "serves hundreds of hospitals and health systems across the country" https://www.criadv.com/industry/hospitals-health-systems/ ; older PDF says "CPA partner of more than 65 hospital and health system clients" (c. 2021, stale) https://www.criadv.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Hospital-Health-Systems-and-Healthcare.pdf
  - Warren Averett: "hundreds of hospitals, nursing facilities and physician practices as clients" (page dated 2017, stale) https://warrenaverett.com/industries/healthcare/physician-practices/
  - HORNE: Cause IQ identifies 111 nonprofit clients, "most ... are hospitals, assisted living..." in MS/LA/TN (third-party estimate) https://www.causeiq.com/vendors/horne-llp,4471164484030361062/ ; HORNE won the Mississippi Division of Medicaid Rural Health Transformation Program application contract (NTE $150,000, Aug-Dec 2025) https://medicaid.ms.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Contract_Award_Analysis_Rural-Health.pdf

  - For scale reference, v1 firm CLA says it "works with more than 900 hospitals, including critical access and rural PPS hospitals" (2025-07-21) https://www.claconnect.com/en/resources/articles/25/rural-health-care-financial-improvements
- **Speed to first deal (judgment):** 3-9 months. A regional firm's healthcare partner can make a warm CFO intro quickly, but formal alliances require risk/independence review and a partner sponsor. Fastest path is advisory (non-attest) practice leaders who already sell revenue-cycle assessments and need an "implementation" answer for denials.
- **Competitor risk:** Mostly **low-medium**. CPA firms advise on denials but do not generally run outsourced appeals. Exceptions: **PYA** (offers "Denials and Appeals Management" and "Revenue Management Overwatch ... helps manage denials" https://www.pyapc.com/insights/case-study-pya-experts-use-analytics-tools-to-remediate-revenue-impacts/) and **LBMC** (denial management plus "Interim Leadership" and "Resource Support") = medium. Big advisory firms are starting AI-denials alliances: **FTI Consulting + Penguin Ai** (Dec 2025), integrating Penguin's AI denial and appeals workflow into FTI RCM engagements https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/penguin-ai-and-fti-unite-expertise-to-deliver-next-generation-revenue-cycle-performance-302644649.html . That is the template (and the competitive threat) for a CPA-firm alliance.

## 2. Interim leadership and staffing firms (interim CFOs, revenue cycle directors)

- **What it is / how hospitals buy:** Boards/CEOs contract interim CFOs or interim revenue-cycle/PFS directors when a leader leaves, during turnarounds or EHR conversions. Often bundled in a management contract (Ovation, CHC, HealthTechS3) or placed by search firms (AMN/B.E. Smith, WittKieffer) or boutiques (Galileo, HARTZ, Nearterm, Tatum). The interim leader is frequently the de facto buyer of revenue-cycle tools for 3-12 months (judgment).
- **Economics:** HealthTechS3 management fee at WhidbeyHealth (CAH) was $350,000/yr plus interim CEO salary at $370,000/yr rate (2022) https://www.whidbeynewstimes.com/2022/02/25/hospital-to-hire-management-firm-interim-ceo/ . AMN says average pay for interim CFO jobs it filled is $305k/yr and it has "helped fill 15 Chief Financial Officer jobs" (undated) https://www.amnhealthcare.com/careers/leadership/apply/interim-chief-financial-officer-leadership-leadership-jobs/ . No vendor referral programs found at any interim firm (NOT_FOUND).
- **Reach (numbers):**

## 3. Coding, CDI and physician-advisor firms serving small hospitals

- **What it is / how hospitals buy:** CAHs outsource coding (backlogs, vacancies), coding audits and education, CDI, and remote physician-advisor (UR second-level review, peer-to-peer). These vendors see denial root causes daily; appeals are the adjacent step. Bought by HIM director / CFO on per-chart, hourly or monthly retainer (Pinnacle "per chart and hourly pricing" https://askphc.com/services/enterprise-risk-healthcare-operations-management-support/).
- **Economics:** Per-chart/hourly (Pinnacle); HIA sells a flat "Critical Access Package" for coding support + audits + education "for one affordable fee" https://hiacode.com/critical-access-hospitals . Fractional physician advisors bill per peer-to-peer case or hourly https://www.fractionalphysicianadvisor.com/service-options . No referral programs found at these firms (NOT_FOUND).
- **Reach (numbers):** HIA ~134 employees per LinkedIn, clients from CAHs to large systems https://linkedin.com/company/health-information-associates-inc- ; Revint (now "an R1 company") "partners with over 1,700 healthcare organizations" per LinkedIn https://linkedin.com/company/revint-solutions ; Sound Physicians had 125+ physician advisors (2022) https://www.soundphysicians.com/press-release/sound-physicians-advisory-services-awarded-best-in-klas/ ; AGS Health 12,000 staff, 150+ customers skewing large systems https://www.agshealth.com/news/ags-health-launches-artificial-intelligence-platform-for-end-to-end-revenue-cycle-management/ ; ArchPro Coding offers the only national CAH coding certification (CAH-CBS) and is sponsored by "many state and national rural/primary care associations" https://www.archprocoding.com/rural-health-coding-billing-specialist-rh-cbs- ; ICAHN (Illinois CAH network) sells remote coding to rural hospitals https://icahn.org/services/remote-coding-support-and-audit-services/
- **Speed (judgment):** 2-6 months for coding-only boutiques (HIA, HMI, ArchPro) that do not appeal; larger firms that already appeal will not refer.
- **Competitor risk:** Mostly **high**. Many sell denials/appeals: Aspirion (AI-drafted appeals, contingency) https://www.aspirion.com/ai-in-denials-management-from-concept-to-reality-blog/ ; AGS Health ("appeals writing", Black Book 2026 top clinical denial vendor) https://www.agshealth.com/services/patient-financial-services/ar-management-services/denial-management-and-prevention-services/ ; Enjoin (Denial Defense, physician-led appeals) https://www.enjoincdi.com/solutions/denial-defense/ ; Sound Physicians (peer-to-peer, appeals) https://careers.soundphysicians.com/en/physician-advisory-services ; Revint (R1) ; himagine (LinkedIn page now presents as Omega Healthcare, which lists "Denials & Appeals Management") https://linkedin.com/company/himagine-solutions ; UASI (establishes/manages denials programs) https://www.uasisolutions.com/denials-revenue-integrity-services ; Pinnacle (edit and denials management) ; VEE HealthTek, GoSourceMD, iMagnum, SMK Coding (all list denial management for CAHs). **Low**: HIA ("We do what we do best - coding") https://hiacode.com/about ; HMI (CAH coding/HIM) https://hmi-corp.com/who-we-serve/critical-access-hospitals.html ; ArchPro (education/certification only).
- **Partner motions:** refer (coding-only firms), endorse/educate (ArchPro curriculum, webinars), integrate (pre-bill tools: MDaudit/eValuator hands denials insight downstream).
- **Taken categories:** Streamline Health acquired by MDaudit (closed 2025) https://streamlinehealth.net/bridging-the-gap-how-evaluator-complements-pre-bill-coding-and-cdi-programs/ ; Aviacode owned by GeBBS (2021) https://gebbs.com/news/chryscapital-backed-gebbs-healthcare-acquires-aviacode/ ; Revint owned by R1 ; Ensemble partnered with Solventum on autonomous coding (2025) https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/05/20/3084933/0/en/Ensemble-and-Solventum-Partner-to-Advance-Autonomous-Inpatient-Coding-with-AI.html ; Healthcare Resource Group (HRG, Spokane) sells CBO/EBO, coding, CDI and had a HomeTown Health (rural network) relationship (2017, via LeadIQ, weak) https://leadiq.com/c/healthcare-resource-group-inc/5a1d842424000024005e9f95
- **Vetting criteria:** NOT_FOUND.
