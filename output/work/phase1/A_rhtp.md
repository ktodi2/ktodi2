(Work in progress; being updated as research proceeds. Research date 2026-09-24.)

## Federal/state funding: CMS Rural Health Transformation Program

### 1. What it is (verified)
- Statute: Section 71401 of Public Law 119-21 (H.R. 1, "One Big Beautiful Bill Act"; CMS calls it the "Working Families Tax Cuts legislation"), signed July 4, 2025. [https://www.medicaid.gov/resources-for-states/rural-health] [https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/press-releases/cms-announces-50-billion-awards-strengthen-rural-health-all-50-states] [MN NOGO: https://www.web.health.state.mn.us/facilities/ruralhealth/ruraltrans/docs/hospitalnogo.pdf]
- Size / years: $50B total, $10B per year FY2026-FY2030; cooperative agreements with states only. [CMS press release above]
- Split: 50% distributed equally among approved states (so ~$100M baseline per state in FY26), 50% allocated by CMS on rurality metrics, state policy actions, and application impact scoring. [CMS press release] [https://pcghealthpolicy.com/2026/01/29/rural-health-transformation-program-applications-awarded-overview-of-state-funding/]
- Timing: NOFO opened Sept 15, 2025; single application window closed Nov 5, 2025; awards announced Dec 29, 2025 (all 50 states). FY27 award amounts to be determined by Oct 31, 2026, then Oct 31 each year through 2029. Budget period 1 is 10 months; later ones 12 months. [https://www.cms.gov/files/document/rural-health-transformation-frequently-asked-questions.pdf] [NOFO: https://apply07.grants.gov/apply/opportunities/instructions/PKG00291485-instructions.pdf]
- Spend deadline / clawback mechanics: states have until the end of the following fiscal year to spend each budget period's award; "unexpended or unobligated funds will be redistributed"; earmarked-but-unpaid subawards do not count as spent; anything unexpended as of Oct 1, 2032 returns to Treasury. State policy commitments must be met by Dec 31, 2028 or scoring (and future funding) suffers. [NRHA NOFO summary: https://nrha-prod-eastus-be.azure.silvertech.net/NationalRuralHealth/media/Documents/Advocacy/2025/RHTP-NOFO-summary-9-16-25.pdf]
- Eligibility / subawards: only the 50 states apply. "Local governments, hospitals, universities, nonprofits ... may still be able to participate through subawards or partnerships if their State chooses." "Organizations should follow the guidance of their Governor's office." [CMS FAQ above]

### Allowable uses (statute categories A-K, NOFO)
States must use funds for at least 3 of: A prevention/chronic disease; B provider payments; C consumer tech solutions ("consumer-facing, technology-driven solutions for the prevention and management of chronic diseases"); D training and TA "for the development and adoption of technology-enabled solutions that improve care delivery in rural hospitals, including remote monitoring, robotics, artificial intelligence, and other advanced technologies"; E workforce; F IT advances ("technical assistance, software, and hardware for significant information technology advances designed to improve efficiency, enhance cybersecurity capability development, and improve patient health outcomes"); G appropriate care availability ("right size their health care delivery systems"); H behavioral health; I innovative care / value-based; J capital expenditures and infrastructure; K fostering collaboration ("improve financial stability of rural facilities"). [NOFO: https://apply07.grants.gov/apply/opportunities/instructions/PKG00291485-instructions.pdf]

Note (judgment): Category F ("software ... designed to improve efficiency") and K ("improve financial stability") are the hooks for back-office/RCM tech; category D explicitly names AI but is framed around "care delivery". The NOFO's own "Financial solvency strategies" section asks states how they will stabilize rural hospitals financially.

### Caps / restrictions
- Provider payments (B) <= 15% of a state's award per budget period. [NOFO]
- EMR replacement <= 5% of award if a HITECH-certified EMR was in place as of Sept 1, 2025. [NOFO]
- Capital/infrastructure (J) <= 20%; state admin <= 10%. [CMS webinar slides via NRHA: https://www.ruralhealth.us/nationalruralhealth/media/documents/advocacy/2025/sapra-presentation-nrha-09-25-25.pdf]
- "Rural Tech Catalyst Fund"-like initiatives <= lesser of 10% or $20M. [NOFO]
- No supplanting: cannot pay for things already funded (e.g., existing software licenses per MN guidance); cannot pay for billable clinical services. [NOFO; MN NOGO]
