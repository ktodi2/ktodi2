## HEADLINE FINDINGS (read first)
1. **Both target EHRs now ship native AI appeal drafting.** MEDITECH launched "Claim Denial Agents" at HIMSS26 (Mar 2026): researches root cause, prioritizes by success-likelihood, "drafts an evidence-based appeal with clinical data extracted from the EHR" [https://businesswire.com/news/home/20260310271908/en/MEDITECHs-Latest-AI-Innovations-Support-Smarter-Care-and-Stronger-Performance]. Oracle Health announced 5 RCM AI tools on 2026-09-23 incl. "Appeal management: identifies payment variances in remittances and automates appeal packet creation", GA "in the coming months" [https://beckershospitalreview.com/finance/revenue-cycle-management/oracle-health-targets-denials-with-5-new-revenue-cycle-ai-tools]; Oracle docs (2026-06-10) already describe a "Denial agent" in "Oracle Autonomous Reimbursement" that composes appeal packets for Cerner Patient Accounting [https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/health/claims-prior-authorizations-payments/demug/manage-denial-appeals-from-cerner-patient-accounting.html]. => The EHR vendors themselves are now competitor_risk = high for the "integrate/marketplace" motion; Aegis positioning must be vs. native agents (judgment).
2. **SSI Group is "the exclusive claims management and eligibility partner for Meditech as a Service (MaaS) clients"** (per SSI's MEDITECH vendor page) [https://ehr.meditech.com/vendors/ssi-healthcare-revenue-cycle-solutions]. MaaS is the model rural MEDITECH hospitals are choosing, so SSI touches the rural MaaS cohort by default. SSI also markets denial management incl. "track appeal deadlines" [https://thessigroup.com/services/denial-management-in-medical-billing/] => medium competitor risk.
3. **Craneware sells RN-written appeal services** ("review denied claim(s), write appeal letter(s)... testify at ALJ hearings") [https://thecranewaregroup.com/solutions/revenue/appeal-services/] and has ~40% of US hospitals as customers [https://thecranewaregroup.com/media/tesflhm2/annual-report-for-the-year-ended-30-june-2025.pdf] => high reach, high competitor risk.
4. **Montana tie:** Livingston HealthCare (Livingston, MT) moved patient balances to ClearBalance from 2026-08-10 [https://livingstonhealthcare.org/news-articles/2026/july/livingston-healthcare-announces-new-patient-paym/].
