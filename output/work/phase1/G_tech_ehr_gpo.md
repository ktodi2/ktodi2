Author: research subagent G. Date: 2026-09-24. Scope: (1) clearinghouses, patient-payment and other RCM tech; (2) MEDITECH / Oracle Health implementation, managed-services, hosting firms and EHR partner programs; (3) GPOs and purchasing co-ops not covered in v1.

## HEADLINE FINDINGS (read first)
1. **Both target EHRs now ship or have announced native AI appeal drafting.** This is the biggest change for the integrate/marketplace motion.
   - MEDITECH launched "Claim Denial Agents" at HIMSS26 (2026-03-10). Per MEDITECH, the agent "researches the root cause, identifies denial trends, and uses success-likelihood modeling to prioritize staff efforts. It then generates a proposed action plan and drafts an evidence-based appeal with clinical data extracted from the EHR" [https://businesswire.com/news/home/20260310271908/en/MEDITECHs-Latest-AI-Innovations-Support-Smarter-Care-and-Stronger-Performance]. MEDITECH's RCM page now lists "Denials & Appeals ... drafting appeals" [https://ehr.meditech.com/ehr-solutions/meditechs-revenue-cycle].
   - Oracle Health announced 5 RCM AI tools on 2026-09-23 (yesterday). One is "Appeal management: identifies payment variances in remittances and automates appeal packet creation", embedded natively, GA "in the coming months" [https://beckershospitalreview.com/finance/revenue-cycle-management/oracle-health-targets-denials-with-5-new-revenue-cycle-ai-tools]. Oracle docs dated 2026-06-10 already describe a "Denial agent" in "Oracle Autonomous Reimbursement" that "composes an appeal packet" for Cerner Patient Accounting claims [https://docs.oracle.com/en/industries/health/claims-prior-authorizations-payments/demug/manage-denial-appeals-from-cerner-patient-accounting.html].
   - Implication (judgment): the EHR vendors are now direct competitors on appeal drafting. Aegis should frame itself as the outcome/throughput layer (writes AND files, measured on dollars recovered) rather than "an appeal letter generator", and should expect EHR sales reps to push the native agent.
2. **SSI Group says it is "the exclusive claims management and eligibility partner for Meditech as a Service (MaaS) clients"** [https://ehr.meditech.com/vendors/ssi-healthcare-revenue-cycle-solutions]. MaaS is the model rural MEDITECH hospitals are choosing (15 rural hospitals picked Expanse in 2025; 250+ rural sites on Expanse, per MEDITECH [https://ehr.meditech.com/news/rural-hospitals-select-meditech-expanse-to-stay-independent-amidst-challenging-landscape]). So SSI touches nearly every rural MaaS site. SSI also sells denial management that can "track appeal deadlines" [https://thessigroup.com/services/denial-management-in-medical-billing/]. Competitor risk: medium (judgment: SSI is workflow and denial tracking, not outsourced appeal writing).
3. **A live rural co-op RFP for denials management (Washington).** Rural Health Enterprise (RHE, owned by The Rural Collaborative and 24 rural WA hospitals) issued an RFP in March 2026 for "Denials Management ... Appeal letter preparation, Submission of appeals" plus underpayments and credit balances. It asks for "group purchasing pricing based upon RHE volume" and an "RHE master services agreement, inclusive of program support administration fee." The network is 31 hospitals (28 CAHs, 3 PPS). EHRs: Epic Community Connect 13, Oracle/Cerner CommunityWorks 7, MEDITECH 8, CPSI 2, Athena 1 [https://ruralcollaborative.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/RHE-RFP-contract-compliance-denial-mgmt-credit-bal.pdf]. This is the clearest example found of a "contract vehicle" for denials at rural scale. Status of the award: NOT_FOUND. (Cross-reference: file E covers The Rural Collaborative as a network.)
4. **Craneware sells RN-written appeal services.** Per Craneware, its nurses "review denied claim(s), write appeal letter(s), compile support documentation and testify when necessary at ALJ hearings" [https://thecranewaregroup.com/media/utlo12fr/fs_appeal-services.pdf]. Craneware also claims about 40% of US hospitals as customers [https://thecranewaregroup.com/media/tesflhm2/annual-report-for-the-year-ended-30-june-2025.pdf]. So it has huge reach and high competitor risk.
5. **Montana ties:** (a) MHA Ventures (Montana Hospital Association) runs a group purchasing program "Powered by Premier, OnHand & Texas Health Resources" [https://mtha.org/business-solutions/mha-ventures-inc/group-purchasing/]. Its endorsed-services brochure lists a "Revenue Cycle" vendor category [https://mtha.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/2025-Ventures-Brochure-Online-Version.pdf]. (b) Livingston HealthCare (Livingston, MT) moved patient balances to ClearBalance starting 2026-08-10 [https://livingstonhealthcare.org/news-articles/2026/july/livingston-healthcare-announces-new-patient-paym/]. (c) Yellowstone High Value Network (24 MT CAHs, run by Cibolo Health) plans to "lower the cost of purchasing equipment and services" [https://businesswire.com/news/home/20250515709279/en/Yellowstone-High-Value-Network-Launches-With-24-Critical-Access-Hospitals-Across-Montana].
6. **Installed base in CAHs by EHR.** These counts come from a third-party analysis of CMS Promoting Interoperability 2024 data plus POS Q4 2025 (n = 1,058 reporting CAHs): Epic 390, Oracle Health 263, MEDITECH 166 (about 119 on Expanse), TruBridge 164. South Dakota is "MEDITECH country at 18 of 37" [https://healthtechauthority.com/what-ehr-systems-are-critical-access-hospitals-actually-using-data-driven-look-cah-ehr-landscape]. Treat these as a secondary source and verify before quoting externally. Oracle CommunityWorks: "more than 300 hospitals in 46 states" per Oracle (2023, stale) [https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/community-health-systems-tap-oracle-to-help-improve-patient-care-301864389.html]. KLAS 2026: Oracle 21.9% of acute hospitals, down a net 56 hospitals in 2025; MEDITECH 14.7%; 84% of legacy MEDITECH deciders chose Expanse [https://fiercehealthcare.com/health-tech/epic-continues-grow-ehr-market-share-it-makes-gains-small-health-systems].

## 1. Clearinghouses, patient-payment and other RCM technology companies

- **What it is / how hospitals buy through it.** Rural hospitals already license a clearinghouse and claims scrubber (SSI, Inovalon/ABILITY, Waystar, FinThrive, Quadax, TriZetto) plus a patient-pay or financing vendor (AblePay, ClearBalance, CarePayment, PatientPay). Many are listed in the MEDITECH Alliance, where Collaborator/Accelerator licenses are signed "directly between the Expanse customer and the Alliance member" [https://ehr.meditech.com/ehr-solutions/meditech-alliance]. A partner in this category could refer Aegis, bundle it, or resell it on its own paper.
- **Typical economics.** No published referral or rev-share rates were found for these vendors (NOT_FOUND). TriZetto Provider Solutions runs a "Channel Partner Program" that partners with "EMR vendors, practice management systems, consultants, associations, GPOs, ACOs" [https://trizettoprovider.com/who-we-serve/partners]; terms NOT_FOUND. FinThrive has a "Partners & Resellers" program and quotes the CereCore CEO on its page [https://finthrive.com/markets/partners-resellers]. Inovalon's "Strategic Partner Ecosystem" offers "Co-marketing, marketplace listing, joint customer" models for EHR/PM and billing-service partners [https://inovalon.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/INS-23-2068-Insights-MORE2-Update-Strategic-Partner-v2.pdf].
- **Realistic reach into rural/CAH/community hospitals.**
  - Craneware: about 40% of US hospitals [annual report above]; KLAS commenters (Apr 2026) mention recommending Trisus Chargemaster to other CAHs [https://klasresearch.com/comments/the-craneware-group-trisus-chargemaster/1809].
  - FinThrive: "Three out of five U.S. hospitals and health systems" per FinThrive [https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/finthrive-debuts-denials-and-underpayments-analyzer-a-unified-solution-for-denials-and-underpayments-at-hfma-2025-302487716.html]. It also sells a rural-sized bundle, "Community Advantage", and markets RHTP funding for it [https://finthrive.com/community-advantage].
  - AblePay: endorsed by 17 state hospital associations, active in 22 states (per SDAHO, Jan 2024, stale) [https://sdaho.org/2024/01/18/partner-demo-webinar-addressing-the-patient-affordability-gap/]. It is also listed by THA, NHA Services, OHA, HASC (2026) and IHA [https://hasc.org/2026/01/19/ensure-strong-patient-collections-with-ablepay/].
  - Inovalon: CAH case studies (Phelps Memorial, NE) and an AHA rural roundtable [https://inovalon.com/resource/case-study-phelps-memorial-health-center-optimizes-revenue-cycle-with-automation-and-data-insights/].
  - SSI: exclusive for MaaS (see headline).
  - PatientPay: started with 3 Texas community hospitals in 2024 and partners with Azalea Health, which serves rural providers [https://businesswire.com/news/home/20240917369629/en/PatientPay-Partners-with-Azalea-Health-to-Improve-Financial-Outcomes-for-Community-Hospitals-and-Medical-Groups].
  - ClearBalance: partnered in June 2026 with HARS, a consultancy that is a strategic partner of TORCH (Texas rural hospitals) [https://clearbalance.org/clearbalance-healthcare-partners-with-healthcare-advanced-revenue-strategies-hars/].
- **Speed to first deal (judgment).** Patient-pay firms (AblePay, ClearBalance, PatientPay) have no appeals product. They sell to the same CFO and already have association endorsements, so a co-referral could close in 60 to 120 days. Clearinghouses and scrubbers have their own denial modules and long partner-review cycles: 6 to 12 months or more.
- **Competitor risk.**
  - High: Craneware (appeal services); FinThrive (denial management suite plus "Denials and Underpayments Analyzer") [prnewswire above]; AKASA (AI Advisor "helps your team write high-quality appeal letters") [https://akasa.com/solutions/ai-advisor/]; Adonis, Janus Health (Claim Intelligence automates "appeal packages") [https://janus-ai.com/accelerate-iq/claim-intelligence/]; VisiQuate (Ana agents for "escalation and appeals", denial overturn prediction) [https://visiquate.com/ana]; ANKA Health (targets CAHs; "Appeals written, submitted, and tracked") [https://ankahealth.ai/hospitals/]; Revecore (launched AI denial appeals in Apr 2026; "over 1,300 hospitals") [https://diagnosticsworldnews.com/news/2026/04/29/revecore-launches-ai-powered-underpayment-recovery-and-denial-appeals-capabilities].
  - Medium: SSI, Inovalon (denial tracking and analytics in RCM Intelligence), MDaudit (Denials Predictor is pre-bill prevention, not appeal writing) [https://mdaudit.com/solutions/revenue-integrity/denials-predictor/], Infinx (medical-necessity agent, beta) [https://infinx.com/medical-necessity-ai-agent/], Thoughtful AI (now part of Smarter Technologies, per LinkedIn seen 2026-09-24) [https://linkedin.com/company/thoughtful-ai].
  - Low: AblePay, ClearBalance, CarePayment, PatientPay (patient-pay only).
- **Partner motions that apply:** refer (patient-pay firms, co-selling to the CFO), integrate (clearinghouse 835/277 data feed), resell (TriZetto, FinThrive and Inovalon programs), endorse (none).
- **Example orgs (sourced rural fact each):** AblePay (endorsed by 17 state HAs); SSI (MaaS exclusive); Inovalon (Phelps Memorial CAH case study); ClearBalance (Livingston MT); FinThrive (Community Advantage for rural); TriZetto (channel program open to associations and GPOs).
- **Existing RCM/denials vendors already embedded (taken categories).**
  - MEDITECH Alliance RCM members found: SSI (claims and eligibility, MaaS exclusive), Waystar, Inovalon Claims Management Pro [https://ehr.meditech.com/node/4973], FinThrive Claims Manager and Insurance Verifier [https://finthrive.com/lp/meditech-partner], Quadax (joined Aug 2025) [https://quadax.com/quadax-joins-meditech-alliance/]. No denials-appeal-specific Alliance member was found in these searches; the roster page did not render. Partial NOT_FOUND.
  - Premier GPO holds a "revenue cycle solutions" category; e.g., AllianceOne (early-out, collections, insurance follow-up) [https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/allianceone-receivables-management-awarded-3-year-revenue-cycle-solutions-contract-with-healthcare-improvement-company-premier-inc-301993406.html].
- **Vetting criteria.** Not published for referral partners (NOT_FOUND). Oracle and MEDITECH marketplace criteria are in Section 2.
- **Notes / surprises.**
  - MEDITECH's own RCM flyer: "97% of our Expanse customers use our RCM tools" [https://home.meditech.com/en/d/mktcontent/otherfiles/revenuecycleflyer.pdf]. Native Claim Denial Agents therefore reach most of the base by default.
  - ClearBalance claims a KLAS survey showed "25% reduction in denial rates" [https://clearbalance.org/healthcare-providers/]. This is an odd self-claim for a financing firm; treat it as marketing.
  - Office Ally, Claim.MD, Cedar and Flywire were not researched in depth. They are mostly physician-practice or large-system focused (judgment), so they are low priority.

## 2. MEDITECH / Oracle Health implementation, managed-services and hosting firms, and EHR partner programs

- **What it is / how hospitals buy through it.**
  - MEDITECH Alliance has five solution types: Innovator, Accelerator, Collaborator, Consulting Services and Infrastructure Services [https://ehr.meditech.com/ehr-solutions/meditech-alliance]. It had 50+ members by Dec 2025 [https://blog.meditech.com/inflection-point-interoperability-shifts-into-a-new-gear].
  - The Consulting Services program has 15 firms, and 400+ consultants completed MEDITECH Foundations training in 2024-25 [https://ehr.meditech.com/news/meditech-alliance-consulting-program-helps-customers-reach-their-full-ehr-potential].
  - Greenfield Workspace is MEDITECH's API sandbox, with 350+ vendors onboarded in 2024 [https://blog.meditech.com/full-speed-ahead-interoperability-at-meditech-enters-a-new-era]. Greenfield tiers: casual developer; certified developer (joint promotion, "does not include shared revenue"); co-developer ("white labeled ... joint marketing and contracting, and shared revenue") [https://ehr.meditech.com/ehr-solutions/greenfield-tiers].
  - "Traverse" is MEDITECH's interoperability network (Traverse Exchange), not an app marketplace. It covers 700+ US facilities in 41 states [https://ehr.meditech.com/ehr-solutions/meditech-interoperability].
  - Oracle Health: the Developer Program and code Console [https://code-console.cerner.com/], then Oracle PartnerNetwork Healthcare Track, then "Oracle Validated Integration", then a listing on the Oracle Healthcare Marketplace [https://blogs.oracle.com/oraclemarketplace/benefits-of-getting-a-fhir-app-validated-and-listed-with-oracle-health]. Oracle also closes deals for third-party RCM tools "in partnership with Oracle" (Streamline Health RevID, 2024) [https://investor.streamlinehealth.net/news-releases/news-release-details/streamline-healthr-announces-new-revidtm-contract-400-bed-health].
- **Typical economics.**
  - Oracle: OPN membership $500 a year, Healthcare Track $3,000 a year, Millennium environment access $5,000 a year ("subject to change") [https://oracle.com/health/developer/api/].
  - MEDITECH Alliance fees: NOT_FOUND.
  - Consulting firms: referral or resale terms NOT_FOUND. Judgment: firms like Tegria, Haffty, HPG and medSR would take a referral fee or subcontract. Aegis's 15% first-year referral model is the natural offer.
- **Realistic reach.**
  - Tegria: MEDITECH clients in the US, Canada and UK, including CAHs. KLAS 2026 "Highest-Ranked MEDITECH Application Hosting Firm" [https://tegria.com/what-we-do/meditech-ehr-services/]; case: Pender Community Hospital (NE CAH) AR backlog [https://blog.meditech.com/rethinking-productivity-to-enhance-revenue-cycle-performance].
  - Acmeware: 100+ MEDITECH hospitals [https://acmeware.com/about].
  - Interbit Data: "more than 750 worldwide customers" (per LinkedIn, seen 2026-09-24) [https://linkedin.com/company/interbit-data-inc.].
  - JTS Health Partners: "More than half of JTS clients operate on Oracle Health" and it does CommunityWorks work [https://jtshealthpartners.com/hit/cerner-communityworks-best-practices-for-pre-and-post-implementation/].
  - medSR: 100+ MEDITECH conversions; La Paz Regional (AZ CAH) MaaS [https://medsr.com/].
  - Haffty: MaaS implementations at San Carlos Apache, Loretto, Thorek [https://hafftyconsulting.com/article/haffty-joins-meditech-consulting-services-alliance-program].
- **Speed to first deal (judgment).** A referral from a consulting firm working a MaaS go-live or a post-go-live AR backlog could produce a deal in about 3 to 6 months. Becoming an Alliance member or Oracle validated app takes 6 to 12 months or more, and both EHRs now have competing native agents, which may slow or block approval.
- **Competitor risk.**
  - High: MEDITECH itself and Oracle Health itself (see headline). Oracle also runs RevWorks, which offers RCM managed services including denial management (secondary source) [https://empocorp.com/business/revworks-business-line-of-the-cerner-corporation.html], and a "CommunityWorks Revenue Cycle Optimization team" [https://oracle.com/asean/customers/pershing-health-system/]. Tegria sells "Outsourced Revenue Cycle Management" [tegria URL above]. JTS sells Extended Business Office (EBO) services to CAHs [https://jtshealthpartners.com/healthcare-management-consulting-for-rural-hospitals-and-critical-access-hospitals/].
  - Medium: medSR ("Claims, Denials & Collections Support"), CereCore ("reduce denials"), RSM.
  - Low: Acmeware, Interbit, Healthcare Triangle, HPG, Haffty, Healthcare IT Leaders, HealthNET (IT, data and implementation only).
- **Partner motions that apply:** integrate (Greenfield, Oracle Marketplace, Acmeware OneLink), refer (consulting firms), resell (Greenfield co-developer tier; Oracle co-sell precedent with Streamline).
- **Example orgs (rural fact each):** Tegria (Pender CAH), medSR (La Paz CAH MaaS), JTS (CommunityWorks CAH clients; Sweeny Community Hospital [https://jtshealthpartners.com/case-studies/sweeny-community-hospital-hit/]), HealthNET Consulting (Sierra Vista Hospital CommunityWorks) [https://healthnetconsulting.com/healthcare-it-consultants/oracle-cerner-consultant/], Nordic (OPN member, CommunityWorks) [https://nordicglobal.com/ehr-services-oracle-health-and-communityworks/], Acmeware (CAHs listed as a client segment).
- **Integration shortcut worth noting.** Acmeware "OneLink" gives third-party vendors data feeds from MEDITECH's Data Repository. Per Acmeware, this is "a channel into the market" for vendors, with existing integrations for "revenue cycle" vendors [https://acmeware.com/vendors], [https://acmeware.com/services/data-integration]. This could be a faster MEDITECH data path than Greenfield (judgment).
- **Taken categories.** MaaS claims and eligibility: SSI (exclusive). MEDITECH native: Claim Denial Agents. Oracle native: appeal management and the Denial agent.
- **Vetting criteria.**
  - Oracle steps: build in the sandbox; enroll in OPN Level 1+ and the Healthcare Path; buy Millennium environment access; complete Validated Integration review; accept OCMA; publish the listing [Oracle blog above].
  - MEDITECH Alliance consulting partners must show "documentation of experience, project management processes, support methodologies, client references, and training" [https://resources.cerecore.net/cerecore-named-consulting-services-partner-in-the-new-meditech-alliance-program].
  - Greenfield: a EULA plus OAuth credentials [https://ehr.meditech.com/ehr-solutions/how-to-work-in-the-greenfield-workspace].
- **Hospital-association-led MaaS cohorts: NOT_FOUND.** The only related items found were a shared Expanse MaaS instance between two Idaho CAHs (St. Mary's and Clearwater Valley) [https://ehr.meditech.com/news/st-mary-s-health-and-clearwater-valley-health-select-meditech-expanse-to-connect-communities] and early MaaS being "Initially offered exclusively to Critical Access Hospitals" (2019, stale) [https://blog.meditech.com/how-maas-promotes-ehr-adoption-for-community-healthcare-systems].
- **Notes.** Healthcare Triangle hosts MEDITECH with CareTech/HTC (sources from 2021-22, stale). "Engage", "Nova", "Park Place", "LabAnswer", "Summit", "Pivot Point", "HCTec" and "CTG" were not verified as MEDITECH/Oracle rural channels in this pass (NOT_FOUND).

## 3. GPOs and purchasing co-ops with rural reach (beyond v1)

- **What it is / how hospitals buy through it.** Vendors win a GPO agreement through an RFI/RFP and a member committee. Members then buy off-contract at pre-negotiated terms. Purchased services (non-supply) are a growing category:
  - Premier: "650+ agreements" in purchased services [https://premierinc.com/providers/group-purchasing-organization].
  - HealthTrust: "more than 260 purchased services contracts", and Valify Solutions Group is its purchased-services GPO with "no cost to join" [https://healthtrustpg.com/thesource/supply-chain-optimization/supply-expense-management/explore-purchased-services-for-additional-cost-savings/].
  - Yankee Alliance: "100 Purchased Services contracts" [https://yankeealliance.com/who-we-serve].
- **Typical economics.** The anti-kickback safe harbor requires a written agreement specifying fees of "3 percent or less" or the actual amount if higher [https://gao.gov/assets/gao-10-738.pdf]. Other data points:
  - Weighted-average vendor admin fees in 2008 were 1.22% to 2.25% (GAO) [same].
  - A 2014 industry report put the average at 1.7%, with about 65% shared back to members [https://supplychainassociation.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/05/Applied_Policy_Report_2014.pdf] (stale).
  - Premier shares admin fees with Acurity members in the "mid-30 percent range" [https://premierinc.com/newsroom/press-releases/premier-inc-to-acquire-acurity-and-nexera-businesses-from-the-greater-new-york-hospital-association].
  - The RHE RFP adds a "program support administration fee" (amount NOT_FOUND).
- **Realistic reach.**
  - HealthTrust: about 1,800 hospitals and health systems (2023) [https://businesswire.com/news/home/20230228006215/en/Healthcare-Organizations-Across-U.S.-Select-HealthTrust-for-Savings-and-Solutions]. Rural access comes through CHC Supply Trust (Community Hospital Corporation), e.g., a 25-bed CAH in McCook, NE [https://chc.com/community-hospital-healthcare-supply-chain-management-services/], and HomeTown Health (Georgia rural network) [https://healthtrustpg.com/thesource/workplace-trends/25-years-of-making-missions-possible/]. RHE (WA) accesses HealthTrust via CHC [https://ruralhealthenterprise.com/contracts].
  - AllSpire Health GPO: 54 acute hospitals; Premier is its national GPO (go-live Jan 2025) [https://premierinc.com/newsroom/press-releases/allspire-health-partners-selects-premier-inc-as-its-national-gpo].
  - Acurity: 300+ hospitals; a Premier company [https://acurity.com/about/].
  - Yankee Alliance: "over 18,400 members"; Premier-aligned [https://yankeealliance.com/].
  - HPS (Michigan): 5,000+ member organizations in 36 states; standalone GPO since 2019 [https://hpsgpo.com/who-is-hps].
  - CHAMPS (Cleveland): 7,000+ locations; Premier partner; says it helps "critical access and rural hospitals" reach better tiers (undated) [https://jhconline.com/full-service-coalition.html].
  - Prairie Health Ventures (NE): 50+ hospitals, "smaller, more rural"; Premier owner [https://phvne.com/about-us/].
  - IPC Group Purchasing (Illinois Health and Hospital Association): Premier plus Capstone [https://ipcgrouppurchasing.com/business-resources/ipc-group-purchasing/become-a-member/].
  - MHA Ventures (MT): Premier/OnHand.
  - Intalere: acquired by Vizient on 2021-04-01 and no longer a separate GPO [https://vizient.com/newsroom/news-releases/vizient-completes-acquisition-of-intalere-expands-its-supply-chain-capabilities]. Provista is Vizient's non-acute GPO [https://icahn.org/news/intalere-is-now-vizient-keeping-members-at-the-center-of-all-they-do/].
- **Speed to first deal (judgment).**
  - National GPO award: 9 to 18 months. HealthTrust starts re-bids "approximately one year before the expiration of existing contracts" [https://healthtrustpg.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/HT_Supplier-Orientation-Guide-2025_20250617.pdf]. GAO found product selection "generally took 6 months" (2003, stale) [https://gao.gov/assets/gao-03-998t.pdf].
  - Regional co-op or network RFP (like RHE): 2 to 6 months.
  - A GPO award is a hunting license, not a deal. It helps most with CFOs who insist on "on contract" buying.
- **Competitor risk.** GPOs themselves: low. But committed-model and national GPOs usually award a category to one or a few vendors. Premier's "revenue cycle solutions" category and R1-via-Premier (per RULES) mean Premier-aligned regionals (AllSpire, Yankee, Acurity, CHAMPS, PHV, IPC, MHA Ventures) may steer members to Premier-contracted RCM vendors: medium risk (judgment).
- **Partner motions that apply:** contract (GPO agreement or regional RFP), endorse (state-association GPO arms such as MHA Ventures and IPC), refer (CHC Supply Trust account teams).
- **Taken categories (sourced):**
  - Premier revenue cycle solutions: AllianceOne (collections, insurance follow-up).
  - Vizient: SpendMend (recovery audit; cost-side, not claims) [https://spendmend.com/spendmend-receives-expanded-contract-agreement-with-vizient-inc/].
  - A denials-appeals award at HealthTrust, Premier or Vizient: NOT_FOUND in these searches.
- **Vetting criteria (sourced).**
  - HealthTrust minimum criteria: meets quality and cost thresholds; "national in scope" distribution and sales force; "financial stability and long term viability"; "must agree to an administrative fee"; single national account manager; submit a Supplier Profile Form (it only contacts you when your category is up for bid) [https://healthtrustpg.com/dl/EC.HT.010.pdf], [https://healthtrustpg.com/suppliers/].
  - Vizient: eSourcing bid calendar and a vendor grievance process [https://prd-vizientinc-renderinghost.azurewebsites.net/who-we-serve/suppliers].
  - Yankee Alliance: member advisory committee awards; "New technologies that demonstrate significant advantages ... are particularly encouraged" [https://yankeealliance.com/suppliers-and-contracts].
  - RHE: CAH/RHC client counts, WA payer experience, BAA, proof of insurance, compliance program and 3-year litigation history [RHE RFP].
- **Notes / surprises.**
  - A small national sales force is a real barrier for HealthTrust ("national in scope ... effective national sales force"). Regional co-ops and rural networks are the more realistic contract vehicles for Aegis in 2026-27 (judgment).
  - The Louisiana Independent Hospital Network Coalition formed a GPO (2023, stale) [https://commonwealthfund.org/publications/2023/may/how-regional-partnerships-bolster-rural-hospitals].
