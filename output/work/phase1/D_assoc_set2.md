Research date: 2026-09-24. All facts carry a source URL. "(judgment)" marks my own inference. Web egress to some association sites (e.g. wyohospitals.com) was blocked for direct fetch; content was read through Exa search and fetch.

## HEADLINE FINDINGS (read first)

1. **Aegis is already a Wisconsin Hospital Association corporate member.** WHA's Corporate Member Directory lists "Aegis Health ... Contact: Krishang Todi, Co-Founder & CEO ... Service: Revenue Cycle Management ... Member Level: Corporate - Associate" [https://wha.org/about-wha/corporate-membership/corporate-member-directory]. Wisconsin has no endorsed-vendor program. It runs a paid corporate-membership ladder instead: Associate, then Patron, then Champion, with M3 Insurance as the single "Premier Partner". The program is run by Leigh Ann Larson [https://wha.org/about-wha/wha-leadership/leigh-ann-larson].
2. **Affiliated Enterprise Solutions (AES, Austin TX; Brian Stevens, President/CEO) is the key multi-state intermediary.** Brian Stevens is the listed contact for Michigan's EBP program, where he is "MHA Business Development Consultant" [https://mha.org/business-services/endorsed-business-partner-program]. He is also the contact for New Hampshire's EBP program [https://nhha.org/membership/endorsed-partners]. AES lists past clients including HASC, California Hospital Share, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Maine, North Dakota, South Dakota, Vermont, WSHA and Wisconsin HA [https://aesllc.org/solutions/association-solutions]. AES says its "Best-In-Class" portfolio of vetted solutions "can be quickly and effectively integrated into your existing Business Partner Program", and Revenue Cycle Management is one of the portfolio categories (same URL). In April 2026 AES launched the **National Hospital Association Strategic Partner Coalition** of metro and regional associations. Named members include Nassau-Suffolk Hospital Council, NorMet (NY), DFWHC, Hospital Council of Northern & Central California and the South Florida Hospital & Healthcare Association [https://linkedin.com/posts/affiliated-enterprise-solutions-llc_healthcareinnovation-hospitalleadership-activity-7445821323504955393-0-gG ; https://waps.l3s.uni-hannover.de/live/https:/www.linkedin.com/company/shiftmed (ShiftMed post, 2026-03)]. AES also sells "channel partner development" and "introductions to decision makers" to vendors [https://aesllc.org/solutions/aes-healthcare-business-solutions]. (judgment) One AES relationship could open a vetting path into many associations at once. That makes AES a candidate channel partner in its own right.
3. **The "taken category" pattern repeats across states.** The same vendors show up again and again. CorroHealth covers denials, coding and AR (MI, IN, HASC-CA, MT, MN, IA, KS, ND). AblePay covers patient payment (HAP-PA, HASC, MA, NH, MT, NE, SD, MN, IA, KS, ND, IN). MCAG covers class-action recovery almost everywhere. **Only a few states endorse an actual AI denial or appeal tool:** Michigan endorsed SmarterDx, whose "SmarterDenials" generates clinical appeal letters (endorsed Nov 2025), and Colorado endorsed LateralCare "revenue alai" (Premier Partner, April 2026). New Jersey endorses Jzanus (a denials-management workflow platform). A dozen or more service arms in this set have **no named denial/appeal vendor**: WSHA, WY, WI, IL, PA, NY (HANYS and Iroquois), ME, NH, MA, MD, VT, CT, AK, HI, DE. (judgment) These are open slots. The strongest are the ones with a rural/CAH base and a formal endorsed program: WA (39 CAHs), WY (16), PA (16), NY/Iroquois (19), ME (16) and NH (13).
4. CorroHealth sells denials and appeals services [https://corrohealth.com/denials]. Under the RULES competitor test that makes it competitor_risk = high wherever it is endorsed. The endorsements do not all cover denials, though. Indiana's endorsement is scoped to "Price transparency products and/or services" [https://partners.ihaconnect.org/_corrohealth]. Kansas's KHSC endorsement covers price transparency (PARA) [https://kha-net.org/AboutKHA/KHSC/KHSCEndorsedServices/CorroHealth]. (judgment) In those states the denials slot may still be technically open.

## State hospital association service arms (set 2 + v1 recheck)

- **What it is / how hospitals buy through it:** Most state hospital associations run a wholly owned for-profit subsidiary or a named program that endorses a small set of vetted vendors, often one per category. Members buy directly from the vendor, and the vendor pays the association. Examples: WHA Resources (WY) [https://wyohospitals.com/resources0240f34b]; Washington Hospital Services, where "When your hospital uses one of our services, the revenue supports the Washington State Hospital Association" [https://wahospitalservices.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/WHS-Preferred-Partners.pdf]; HAPevolve (PA) [https://hapevolve.com]; HANYS Marketplace (NY) [https://hanysmarketplace.com/news/press_releases/2026/2026-03-05-hanys_marketplace_partners_medallion]; AllHealth Inc. (HASC) [https://hasc.org/endorsed-business-partners]; Maine Hospital Solutions [https://themha.org/corporate-affiliates.html].
- **Typical economics (public):**
  - Maryland (MHA Prime): "Endorsed Business Solutions Partner ... Investment: Revenue Share with a commitment to sponsor MHA Annual Meeting". Corporate Affiliate tier is $5,000-$20,000 [https://mhaonline.org/about-maryland-hospital-association/partnerships].
  - North Dakota: "Revenue share earned from Strategic Business Partner relationships helps offset the expenses of NDHA" [https://ndha.org/services/strategic-business-partners].
  - Michigan: "The endorsed business partners pay fees related to the program benefits" [https://mha.org/business-services/endorsed-business-partner-program].
  - Indiana: "the more revenue flows back to IHA" [https://partners.ihaconnect.org/_ihamembers].
  - Kansas: KHSC "provides more than $300,000 annually in direct and indirect resources to KHA" and "endorses 18 different vendor partners" [https://kha-net.org/Communications/CurrentReportPDFs/d175773.aspx?type=view] (undated).
  - No state in this set publishes an exact royalty %. NOT_FOUND.
- **Realistic reach (numbers, CAH counts from Flex Monitoring Team 2024-cost-report table, published Apr 2026)** [https://flexmonitoring.org/sites/flexmonitoring.umn.edu/files/media/state-medians-2024data_report-final_2026.pdf]:
  - Set 2 states: WY 16, WA 39, WI 53, MI 35, IL 53, IN 33, PA 16, NY 19, CA 36, ME 16, VT 8, NH 13, MA 3, AK 13, HI 9. CT, NJ, MD and DE are not in the table, i.e. 0 CAHs.
  - v1 recheck states: MT 49, NE 61, SD 39, MN 75, IA 81, KS 82, ID 26, CO 32, ND 37.
- **Speed to first deal (judgment):** Expect 3-9 months to endorsement. Most programs need an application, due diligence (references, financials, demos), then committee or board approval. Examples: WHS Board review [WSHA PDF above]; "special selection committee comprised of executives from Indiana Hospitals" [https://partners.ihaconnect.org/_prospectivepartners]; RFI and financial checks in MT [https://mtha.org/business-solutions/mha-ventures-inc/endorsed-services]. Wisconsin is faster because Aegis is already a member. Corporate-member tiers and sponsorships can be bought right away, but they are not endorsements.
- **Competitor risk:** Medium to high in states that already endorse CorroHealth for denials (MI, HASC, MN, ND, SD) or SmarterDx (MI). The same goes for LateralCare (CO), which also sells denial management, and for Ovation Healthcare (CO Premier Partner, revenue cycle) [https://cha.com/members-resources/shared-services/premier-partners]. Many programs are exclusive ("We only choose one partner per service" [https://wahospitalservices.com/preferred-partners]; "Endorsements are exclusive" [https://allhealthinc.com/california-hospital-share]). An existing RCM/denials endorsement can therefore block Aegis outright.
- **Partner motions that apply:** endorse (primary), refer (for the service-arm staff), contract (MHA Prime/IPC/UISS purchasing arms), and fund (association foundations; not investigated).
- **Vetting criteria found:**
  - WHS (WA): SME review, background and viability review, calls to other state associations, hospital SME reviews, references, WHS Board review [WSHA PDF].
  - Indiana: must address critical member needs, align with IHA, be unique/innovative, and scale statewide [https://partners.ihaconnect.org/_ihamembers]. Partners also "provide discounts to IHA members as a condition of their endorsement" [https://partners.ihaconnect.org/_prospectivepartners].
  - MHA Ventures (MT): consult members, RFI, proposal review, product demo, financial health check, marketing strategy assessment, customer references [https://mtha.org/business-solutions/mha-ventures-inc/endorsed-services].
  - California Hospital Share: identify need, market scan, board endorsement; exclusive [https://allhealthinc.com/california-hospital-share].
  - NHHA/NDHA/MN/NE: AES-style "deliberate market assessment process". Benefits include an endorsement letter signed by the CEO and introductions to decision makers [https://nhha.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/NHHA_ENDORSED-BUSINESS-PARTNER-PROGRAM_2024-for-Website.pdf].
